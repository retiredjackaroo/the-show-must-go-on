Lucian's memorial song for [[Ordrin Emberkeg]], performed at the [[Belhaven Hunt]] in Session 6. It remembers Ordrin as the company's quartermaster, healer, brewer and stubborn conscience, while opportunistically blaming him for the Belhaven barge explosion.

**Popularity:** 2 — Crowd Favourite (Performance 18)

**Royalties:** 10 gp per session

![[Assets/Ordrin Did It.mp3|Ordrin Did It]]

## Lyrics

Ordrin kept the weapons, Ordrin kept the ale,  
He patched up Thud whenever Thud began to fail.  
He tried to hit a bell-frog, but his aim was rather poor—  
He shot Lucian in the foot and nearly started war.

**Oh, Ordrin, Ordrin, stubborn little sod,**  
**He'd argue with a captain, a host, or any god.**  
**He blew up that old barge—we've all agreed it's true—**  
**And since he can't deny it, the blame will have to do.**

The host told Ordrin, “Sit down. You haven't got a choice.”  
Ordrin threw a Fireball and stopped the bastard's voice.  
Then just to make quite certain that the irritating man was dead,  
Ordrin threw another one and burned him toes to head.

**Oh, Ordrin, Ordrin, stubborn little sod,**  
**He'd argue with a captain, a host, or any god.**  
**He brewed, he healed, he fought—and made a splendid fuss.**  
**So if there's blame to hand around: blame Ordrin, never us.**

A light came down and struck him; he vanished on the spot.  
His loot bag gave a final jingle—that was all we got.  
So raise a mug and drink it dry, then fill it up again:  
To Ordrin Emberkeg—our dwarf, our cleric and our friend.
