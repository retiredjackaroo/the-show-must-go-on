Lucian's Fen Mother ballad, performed at the Belhaven Hunt in Session 3 and written as a swaggering account of the Session 2 victory. The song credits Lucian's strategy for recruiting Arlen Marr, then gives the supporting cast their deliberately exaggerated places in the tale.

![[Assets/Lucian_Wins_the_Battle.mp3|Lucian Wins the Battle]]

## Lyrics

### Verse 1

Come gather close and fill your glass,
I'll tell you how it went:
A monster rose from Greyfen's mire
On murder fully bent.
She bit the hull, she lashed her tail,
She churned the marsh to spray—
But I had killed the Fen Mother
Before we sailed that day.

### Chorus

Oh, steel may strike and hammers fall,
And brave fools lead the fray,
But Lucian wins the battle
While the foe is miles away.
Let Thud rage on, let Ordrin pray,
Let crossbow quarrels fly—
I simply place the pieces, boys,
And tell you where to die!

### Verse 2

At Greyfen stood Lieutenant Marr,
With soldiers at his back;
He came to seize our witness
And reclaim the stolen facts.
I raised one forged retirement sheet,
Then laid his choices bare:
“Come serve the truth—or serve a lord
Who'll kill you when you fail.”

### Verse 3

Now Tavia held the ledger tight,
And Ordrin worked the crowd;
He made the soldiers hear the names
Their masters disallowed.
Thud loomed nearby with Vane's old maul—
A nuanced argument—
And soon I'd turned an enemy
Into our armament.

### Verse 4

Next morning on the ferry deck
The Fen Mother appeared;
Captain Oda drove us on
While lesser souls grew feared.
Jon Hay bent his longbow,
Tavia loosed a bolt;
And Ordrin's spectral warhammer
Came swinging like a ghost.

### Verse 5

I spoke a word of power—“Flee!”—
She rudely disagreed;
So I filled her skull with whispers
Of a darker, deeper breed.
Young Sebastian raised his spear
And paid the soldier's price;
So drink his name with honour, friends—
The marsh is cold as ice.

### Verse 6

Then Marr drew back his heavy bow,
The string sang sharp and low;
One final bolt split hide and bone
And felled the monstrous foe.
“His shot!” you cry. Quite right, my friends—
But answer, if you can:
Who won us that fine crossbow?
Who recruited that fine man?

### Final Chorus

Oh, Thud may swing and Ordrin chant,
And Marr may save the day,
But Lucian wins the battle
While the foe is miles away.
The finest blade is strategy,
The sharpest bolt—a friend;
A genius starts the victory
Before the fight begins!

### Outro

Then Thud threw out the anchor
And hooked her through the hide;
The captain slowed, while Ordrin carved
The carcass open wide.
They took her skull and leather,
Her teeth and meat for pay—
But I had bagged the greatest prize:

_I'd been proved right all day._
