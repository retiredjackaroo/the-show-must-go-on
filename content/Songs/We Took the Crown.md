Lucian's victory song for the party's expedition from Belhaven to the Crown Observatory. It celebrates Ember's fire, Nova's aim, Thud's giant form and Lucian's own deliberately modest guidance.

The Session 9 performance was a new song for a new audience and received a very strong response. Popularity and royalties were deferred for later calculation.
