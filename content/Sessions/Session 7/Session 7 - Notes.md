## The night at camp

Back at the trail camp, the party bought two additional days of mule feed and built a light-screen around the fire. Nova kept watch while the others completed a long rest. Lucian performed a new song for the party, directed knowingly toward an unseen audience; no external response occurred during ordinary continuity.

The [[Surveyor's Compass]] still pointed toward Rusk. The party packed the portable ram, tools, rope, tents, rations, cold-resistance potion and remaining quarry explosives into Nova's extradimensional bag before climbing back toward Windcut Ridge.

## Rusk and the Lament Widow

![[Assets/Session 7 - Rusk and the Lament Widow.jpg|Lucian bargains with the Lament Widow for Rusk.]]

On a cliff shelf above the ridge, the party found Rusk pinned beneath a taloned, woman-shaped harpy called a [[Lament Widow]]. Lucian approached peacefully and traded rations for the dog. He gave Rusk to Thud, who started down toward Kelm, while Lucian remained to share the promised meal.

The Widow treated Lucian's companions as his pets, then became hostile when he objected. Lucian evaded her with invisibility and repeated illusions while Nova fired from cover. Ember arrived by air and Thud returned too late to strike before Lucian killed the Widow with his rapier. The nest and body yielded 155 gp of miscellaneous valuables. Ember recovered one missing Hunt tag below the cliff.

Thud returned Rusk to Kelm. The reward was three Potions of Healing, a climber's kit, 350 gp and the [[Widow-Safe Collar Clip]]. Nova identified the clip; Thud retained ownership but fastened it to Lucian.

## The meridian ring

![[Assets/Session 7 - Meridian Ring Ambush.jpg|The quarry explosives and Ember's Fireball open the battle on the meridian ring.]]

Ember scouted the broken wall, instrument yard and crown. She saw the [[Cry Wildheart]]—the source of the Eastern Cry—fighting a 21-foot frost giant on the meridian ring. The transcript renders the giant's name several ways; this draft uses the provisional spelling Thurnvold. Both creatures had been fighting for almost a day.

The party passed dormant Meridian Wards and prepared an ambush. Lucian made himself and Thud invisible. Thud placed a barrel of quarry explosives between the combatants, then Ember ignited it with Fireball. The blast injured both creatures and drew them into battle with the party.

Thud charged the Cry Wildheart and used [[Grandmother's Grasp]] and the [[Horn of Ramming Speed]] to pin and drive it back. Thurnvold struck both combatants with cold, grappled Thud and hurled him sixty feet. Nova's radiant arrows and force ballista, Ember's fire and Lucian's Dissonant Whispers wore the giant down while the Cry Wildheart repeatedly slipped away from attacks. The Cry eventually retreated to its observatory-roof nest.

Thud killed Thurnvold by smashing his knee; the giant fell onto broken stone and snapped his neck. The party recovered 200 gp, a huge mundane greataxe worth 50 gp, oversized patchwork hide-and-ice armour worth 80 gp and a war horn worth 40 gp. Thud took a molar as a trophy. Its unrevealed magic manifested in the next fight: once per long rest, when Thud rages while attuned, he may become Huge for the rage's duration, gain ten-foot reach and wield weapons normally too large for him.

## The Cry Wildheart's lair

![[Assets/Session 7 - Cry Wildheart Fight.jpg|The party fights the Cry Wildheart on the observatory roof.]]

The party entered the observatory without provoking its Meridian Wards and climbed directly to the roof. The wounded Cry Wildheart opened with a thunderous cry that destroyed Nova's force ballista, heavily wounded the group and deafened Thud and Nova.

The close roof and central opening made the beast's wind attacks especially dangerous. Thud was knocked unconscious by a second full cry; Lucian revived him with Healing Word. Ember's fear effect prevented the creature from advancing toward her and trapped it on the platform. Nova delivered the killing shot with a radiant arrow.

The party recovered:

- Two branching antlers, worth 500 gp each and usable by the Hunt to craft a magic item.
- Four wing membranes, worth 640 gp each.
- Two cry glands, worth 250 gp each.
- Hide scraps worth 50 gp.
- One additional wing membrane and a second missing Hunt tag found during the later search.
- A rib taken by Thud as a trophy. Its effect remains unrevealed; the clue given was that the sound of Thud's hammer strikes echoed across the gap.

## Observatory discoveries

![[Assets/Session 7 - Anomalous Observatory Diagram.jpg|The anomalous numbered astronomical diagram found by Ember.]]

The party camped on the roof and completed an uninterrupted long rest. Ember found a numbered astronomical diagram unlike the magical planes described in her book, _A Primer on the Axioms of Magic and the Known Planes_. Its meaning remains unresolved.

During the rest, the observatory clicked, whirred and surged with magic. Nothing immediately attacked, but the complex felt as if fresh life had been breathed into it. Its Meridian Wards, wretches, lower service areas, instrument yard, cave, weather apparatus and remaining boss have not been resolved.
