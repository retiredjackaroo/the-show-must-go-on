## The upper works and weather vane

![[Assets/Session 8 - Observatory Upper Works Map.jpg|Registered layout of the Crown Observatory's upper works.]]

The party descended from the damaged telescope roof into the upper works. The Meridian Wards tracked them but did not attack until they interfered with protected machinery.

Across a broken exterior bridge, three Chart Riders and several Gap Siphons attacked. When Thud raged while attuned to the frost giant's molar, he became Huge, gained a ten-foot reach and wedged himself between the bridge rails. The party destroyed the attackers without anyone falling.

The cart-sized weather vane at the far pinnacle was a fixed magical climate device. Its settings were arctic, moderate, rainy and volcanic, with a five-day transition between climates. The party changed it from arctic to moderate. Nearby they recovered the third missing Hunt tag, 40 gp of abandoned Hunt equipment and a play/pause remote able to control particular Meridian Wards.

## The chart vault

After Lucian opened a protected hatch, the party fought two Meridian Wards in a lower chart vault. Nova's remote shut down one construct temporarily; after the fight, the party remembered that the Hunt required a deactivated guard and later secured one intact in Nova's extradimensional bag.

The vault contained:

- An Echo Ward schematic that visibly calls for material from the [[Cry Wildheart]]. Its function remains unknown.
- Two additional Meridian Ward remotes.
- The rare [[Wish You Were Here Postcard]].
- Star charts numbered 3 and 23.
- Books, rolled charts, an orrery and stacks of circular brass plates.

The postcard fixes a beach hotel as a known magical destination while it remains active. Once per long rest it can cast Sending to the writer or hotel desk, and once it can teleport its holder and up to five willing nearby creatures to the hotel without mishap; that journey consumes its magic and erases the stored coordinates.

## Two puzzles

The adjacent animal wheel showed a frog, snake, bug, dragon, paw print and wing. The party confirmed that this mechanism was unrelated to the planetary charts. Selecting the wrong animal summoned a hostile Meridian Echo. After several failed choices and two such fights, the party solved the wheel and opened its cache.

The cache held star plates worth 110 gp, completing that Hunt objective, a [[Meridian Log Case]] and further star charts. Hidden charts inside the case brought the party's total to seven: Days 3, 5, 7, 11, 13, 17 and 23. The complete solution remains unknown.

The party also collected 60 gp of frost resin and 25 gp of Constellation Smear from the Meridian Echoes.

## Jeremy and the Crynord

![[Assets/Session 8 - Observatory Ground Floor Map.png|Registered layout of the Crown Observatory's ground floor.]]

In the main hall, the party found numerous human-shaped survivors calling themselves [[Crynord]]. One, Jeremy, remembered working at the observatory before its abandonment roughly forty years earlier. He said the astronomers saw the planets moving incorrectly, reached out through the telescope and attracted the attention of something beyond their gods. The being noticed them and cursed them.

Nova's Detect Magic confirmed an extraordinarily powerful external curse that stopped the Crynord ageing, scattered their minds and left them prone to aggression. The Cry Wildheart had worsened their condition, but that additional influence was fading after its death. Lucian's Dispel Magic failed with a warning that he lacked the required administrative privileges. A cure will require unusually powerful magic.

Jeremy recognised the postcard as his. Its Sending reached an older woman named Sally at the beach hotel, who was still waiting for him.

## Boiler cellar

![[Assets/Session 8 - Observatory Foundation Map.png|Registered layout of the Crown Observatory's foundation rooms.]]

The observatory console reported that two of three major threats had been eliminated. In the boiler cellar, the party found a ruptured boiler approaching catastrophic failure. Three valves had to be aligned correctly. Nova's first guess relieved part of the pressure; Ember found matching notches; Thud and Lucian both turned valves the wrong way and were burned. Nova reversed Thud's setting and stabilised the system.

The strongbox beneath the boiler contained 425 gp, two Potions of Healing, three Potions of Greater Healing, two antitoxins, a brass valve fitting and a keychain with two keys. A small pool of apparently sentient water escaped through the pipes.

## The Braced Melt

The water gathered in the cistern as the [[Braced Melt]], an enormous elemental of water, ice and rubble wearing two magical bronze braces. Nova and Ember destroyed the braces first, stripping away the creature's unusual resistances. The elemental flooded chambers, dragged Nova and Thud into the freezing cistern and battered them at close range. Lucian remained near the doorway, used illusions and support magic, then killed the weakened elemental with Dissonant Whispers.

The party recovered 120 gp of alchemical material. Thud kept a valueless fragment of the destroyed bracing as a trophy.

## A claimed bastion

With all three major threats defeated, the party activated the observatory console and claimed the complex as its main base. The remaining Meridian Wards changed allegiance. The Crynord stayed and may eventually help maintain the site if their curse can be cured.

The bastion includes facilities beyond what the party could normally build at its current level. Its observatory still needs reconstruction. The large ring outside is a disabled teleportation circle; restoring it requires solving the [[Meridian Star Charts]] puzzle.

Lucian ended the session by performing a song about Nova for the observatory's residents and rolled 19 for Performance. The reward and Session 8 XP remain to be calculated.
