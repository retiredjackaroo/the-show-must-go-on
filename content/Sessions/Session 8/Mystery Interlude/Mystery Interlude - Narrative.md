The observatory gears stopped in the middle of a click.

For one instant there was nothing.

Then applause.

Gold light covered a curved studio couch. Cameras drifted between four guests. Beyond them, an audience occupied tiered balconies without moving.

The host sat behind his desk, smiling.

He had been rebuilt.

He welcomed Thud, Lucian and Nova back. Ember received the introduction reserved for a new star.

Thud went first. The host asked whether he ever separated fighting from turning the world around him into a weapon. Thud did not understand the distinction. Plans failed. Explosions provided something useful to do next.

The host asked about the teeth, horns, ribs and tongues.

Memories, Thud said. And fun.

The host wondered whether production should begin collecting souvenirs too.

Ember hid behind Thud. The host recited her recent history as a sequence of Fireballs, then pressed her about Vasilifrax and Vane's hammer. Ember demanded answers. The host offered only the possibility of another storyline.

Lucian called him Dave.

He spoke about singing as Sebastian, turning Ordrin into a song and remaining for dinner with the Lament Widow. When the host asked who Lucian had winked at during his campfire performance, Lucian looked toward the darkness.

Any audience that might be watching.

He believed they noticed him. He wanted them to know that he noticed them too.

Nova sat through praise for her killing blows and questions about control. Ember's magic had forced Nova to flee. The damage mattered less than the theft of agency.

Then came the older question.

Nova remembered being Nova. Nova also remembered being a toad. New victories and new companions made Nova feel more real, but they did not erase what came before.

Did Nova want the answer discovered, or delivered?

All of the answers, Nova said.

At last the host turned to unfinished business. A screen showed Ordrin with his arm extended and fire crossing the studio.

Production had restored the host. The showrunner had returned him to broadcasting duty. Not a hair was out of place.

Had any of them truly expected Ordrin's attack to work?

Lucian answered for himself.

No.

The host smiled. Lucian welcomed him back. Ember watched with disgust.

“Now,” the host said, “let's continue the show.”

The studio faded.

They stood again on the observatory roof.

Lucian warned the others that the host and showrunner held the power. Confronting them directly would be foolish—for now.

Nova changed the subject.

The cameras might still be watching.
