> [!warning] Quarantined continuity
> This record belongs only to the unresolved Mystery Interlude stream. Its events, people, places, rules and implications must not update ordinary campaign entities or appear in the standard Session 8 record.

Lucian Vale, Thud Firebelly, Nova and Ember were taken from the observatory roof to the studio. The restored host interviewed them about their recent decisions, introduced Ember as a first-time guest and revisited Ordrin Emberkeg's earlier attack. The host claimed that production had rebuilt him and returned the four to the roof after closing the broadcast.

## Participants

- Lucian Vale
- Thud Firebelly
- Nova
- Ember
- The host
- The unmoving studio audience

## The restored host

The host opened an “after-show” with a summary of the party's recent adventures. Although the sound of applause filled the studio, Lucian saw that the figures in the balconies were not moving. Later, the host drew attention to the audience while they continued staring forward from the darkness.

The host said he had recovered from Ordrin's attack through production's work and the showrunner's continued confidence. He displayed an image of Ordrin casting Fireball and asked whether the returning guests had expected the attack to work.

## Thud

The host questioned Thud about treating the environment as a weapon and collecting trophies. Thud said fighting and weaponising the surroundings were the same thing. He described trophies as memories and entertainment, and the host suggested the studio might begin collecting souvenirs of its own.

## Ember

Ember hid behind Thud and resisted the interview. The host pressed her on repeated uses of Fireball and asked whether her interest in Vane's hammer belonged to her mission or the party's. When Ember demanded information about Vasilifrax and the hammer, the host said production knew only what it had watched and treated her history as a possible future storyline.

## Lucian

Lucian called the host “Dave” and discussed performing as Sebastian, his memorial song for Ordrin and his encounter with the Lament Widow. He openly described his relationship with the audience as reciprocal: he entertained them and believed they rewarded and watched him in return. After the broadcast, he cautioned the others that the host and showrunner held greater power and that direct confrontation was currently unwise.

## Nova

The host praised Nova's repeated killing blows and questioned how Ember's fear magic had affected Nova's agency. He then returned to Nova's competing memories of being Nova and being a toad. Nova said new experiences made her feel more like Nova without diminishing what came before, and that she wanted to discover all the answers rather than simply be told.

## Return

The host ended by saying, “Now, let's continue the show.” The studio faded and the four found themselves on the observatory roof. Their immediate discussion of the host, audience, cameras, showrunner and Ordrin remains part of this quarantined record.
