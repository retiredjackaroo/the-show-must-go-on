Lucian Vale, Thud Firebelly, Nova and Ember returned from the [[Meridian Hill]] observatory, defeated a professional roadside ambush, collected the completed [[Belhaven Hunt]] rewards and found [[Belhaven]] under [[Lord-Captain Edric Vane|Vane]]'s influence. Lucian and Ember infiltrated the military ward, recovered [[Lucan Rhyse]] and escaped. Lucian then used a new song and public appeal to turn the Hunt against Vane's recruitment drive.

## Return from the Crown

The observatory is now the party's shared bastion. Its observatory and teleportation circle are additional facilities, but both require work: the telescope must be repaired and four future unlabelled star charts must be identified before the circle activates. The weather transition had four days remaining when the party departed.

The party received level 7 and updated Narrative Accommodation values before leaving. Lucian was also granted a provisional **120 gp** in accumulated song income; the DM explicitly deferred the exact calculation.

## Roadside ambush

On the second day of travel, bandits attacked from an abandoned farmstead. Lucian opened with Faerie Fire and distributed Bardic Inspiration. Ember's Fireball destroyed most of the attackers; Nova and Thud fought the surviving captains and crime lord. Lucian killed one captain with a critical bow shot and twice restored Thud with third-level Healing Word. Thud ultimately killed the crime lord.

The party recovered three usable pistols, 60 bullets, three scimitars and one unidentified vial of poison. Lucian kept one pistol. The farm's former occupants were missing; no evidence established whether they fled, were paid off or were killed.

Striking the corpse revealed the power of Thud's Cry Wildheart rib trophy: when he attacks an inanimate object, it can release a 30-foot cone of thunder, with a DC 16 Constitution save, 4d8 thunder damage on a failure or half on success, and temporary deafness on a failed save. The ability recharges on a short rest.

## Belhaven under Vane

Outside Belhaven, makeshift camps held prospective recruits carrying improvised weapons. Soldiers patrolled the camps and recruitment posters called for enlistment against the Hollow Host. The party entered openly as the heroes of Greyfen and delivered its completed Hunt objectives.

The Hunt paid **4,555 gp** in recorded rewards. A Hunt crafter retained the wagon and expedition materials for later crafting proposals.

Local accounts said Vane had cleared the party's name, blamed the barge attack on the Hollow Host and planned to leave for Greyfen with the first recruits in three days. Rhyse had disappeared after an assassination attempt. At the Open Bell sanctuary, [[Karad Ren]] and [[Tavia Rel]] said the military investigation had stalled and suspected Vane had taken him.

## Infiltration and rescue

The [[Surveyor's Compass]] pointed toward Rhyse's familiar clothing in the military ward. Lucian and Ember entered invisibly while Nova and Thud prepared an extraction outside. Lucian saw Vane alive and unscarred, then found Rhyse confined below the compound, suffering nightmares and unable to remember him.

Lucian quietly sang from the party's recent history. Rhyse recognised him and remembered the assassination attempt, though not what followed. Lucian made them both invisible and began the escape. Ember maintained a communication relay; Nova and Thud moved to the roofline. Their movement alerted Vane, and Thud threw three improvised firebombs into the tents as a distraction.

Lucian, Rhyse and Ember crossed the compound. At the final guard, Lucian abandoned concealment, pushed the others toward safety and met Vane's gaze. Vane smiled. Lucian walked out after them rather than running.

Rhyse later recognised that the ledger copy entrusted to him was gone. The party still retains the originals. The DM compared Rhyse's missing memories to Nova's earlier unexplained memory loss and said both appear connected to Vane.

The Session 8 Nova song had previously received **19** at the Crown Observatory, adding +2 Popularity. Its result of **20** at the Hunt records a second distinct audience and adds +3, for 5 total. The Session 9 transcript verbally labels the 21-point venue “Belhaven Church,” but the surrounding scene and later confirmation place the performance at the Hunt; the discrepancy is retained here rather than treated as a second venue.

All four songs are Local Songs. Lucian is level 7, so each pays 10 gp at the end of a session: **40 gp per session total** from these four established Back Catalogue entries.

After _Let Vain Count Glory_, Lucian made a 22 Persuasion check and convinced the Hunt that Vane's recruits were in danger. About half the crowd initially wanted immediate action. Lucian's later 23 tied Thud's opposing 23 and kept them from marching that night. The agreed plan was to discourage enlistment and bring existing recruits home.

## Purchases and obligations

Lucian negotiated a +1 longbow and Bracers of Archery from a shop whose spoken name is uncertain in the transcript, paying 450 gp rather than the quoted 850 gp. In exchange, he promised the shop a favourable mention in a future song. He did not fulfil that sponsorship during the Hunt performance.

Ember bought Boots of Elvenkind for 400 gp. Ember and Thud each bought a Cloak of Protection with a ten-percent multi-purchase discount.
