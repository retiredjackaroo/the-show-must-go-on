At the [[Belhaven Open Bell Sanctuary]], the party gave [[Lucan Rise]] and two military clerks its evidence against [[Lord-Captain Edric Vane]]. Rise agreed to investigate and received a copy of the records; the party retained the originals. The meeting ended without arrest or immediate discharge. That evening the party destroyed the repaired barge during a dockside concert, escaped Belhaven under its registered cover identities and began the Hunt's quarry contract. On the road, the book [[What the Fuck Is Going On]] appeared and disclosed Star Power and Narrative Accommodation. At the [[Northern Quarry]], the party defeated two swarms of [[Bucket Scrabs]], found a [[Surveyor's Compass]] and reached level 5. The ironbark charger remains ahead.

## The sanctuary hearing

[[Lucan Rise]] arrived unarmed with two clerks and framed the meeting as an administrative review. His office had Lucian, Ordrin and Thud recorded as absent without leave; Tavia as a clerk who departed with documents still under reconciliation; and Marr as the officer sent to recover her. Rise said no assault or theft had been reported.

Lucian produced the party's retirement papers. Tavia described taking the [[Altered Dispatch Ledger]] after seeing casualty and movement records altered during the mutiny. Rise read entries showing 43 soldiers and 19 civilians missing corrected to 12 soldiers and no civilians, a diverted southern relief convoy and a duplicated movement slip marked for disposal. He initially treated these as potentially authorised changes to draft records. The party answered that the official figures did not match people and campaigns they had witnessed.

Thud and Nova separately confirmed that Vane had ordered the company against an Elder Dragon. Nova said she had initially supported Vane and sought out the party later because she wanted to understand the company's breakdown.

### The hammer demonstration

Rise recognised [[Vane's Warhammer]] as Vane's weapon but said no theft had been reported. To demonstrate its effect, Thud killed a spider with it after both clerks had recorded seeing the creature. The witnesses immediately forgot the spider despite their notes and the remains on the sanctuary floor. This confirmed the kill-to-forgetting effect to Rise and the clerks; it did not establish a full explanation for Sebastian's different case.

The sanctuary priest then used Zone of Truth. Lucian, Ordrin and Thud were affected; Nova was not. Under the effect, the party repeated its account of the mutiny, the dragon order, the altered records and the hammer. Rise said their testimony gained credibility but still required comparison with official records.

### Terms and unresolved agreement

- Rise said he served the greater military and the Crown rather than Vane personally.
- He reported Vane travelling to Belhaven to deliver news about operations in the far western marches, with arrival expected within three days.
- Rise offered immediate discharge if the party surrendered every original and copy and Tavia and Marr returned to the army. The party refused.
- The party gave Rise a copy for verification and retained the originals until Vane could be brought to justice and their safety secured.
- Rise offered honorable discharge and benefits after investigation even if wrongdoing was not proven, provided the party did not publicly damage the army with unproven allegations.
- Marr was offered a return to service with six months' leave. Tavia was offered the same honorable-discharge option as the party. Neither person's final answer was recorded.
- Ordrin promised not to air the matter publicly. Lucian made the promise conditional on a fair investigation and protection from Vane, reserving recourse to higher civilian authority and the public if those conditions failed.
- Rise left without arresting anyone. Discharge papers and back pay were deferred pending Vane's arrival and the investigation.

## The barge burning

Immediately after the meeting, Thud proposed burning the barge. The party promoted a dockside performance for that evening and bought five gold pieces' worth of air-sensitive contact powder for pyrotechnic arrows. The crowd included Rise, his clerks, the harbourmaster, guards and civilians.

Thud removed his scale mail, posed as a drunk and planted moonshine, all six bottles of his lamp oil and improvised explosives on the barge. Nova took a rooftop position with contact-powder arrows. Ordrin watched from a separate vantage and tried to distract a guard. Lucian performed from an improvised stage.

Nova's first shot safely diverted the guard. The second ignited the prepared barge. Lucian's performance ended before the planned crescendo, leaving an awkward silence in which the vessel exploded. Fire followed Thud's moonshine trail as he ran through the fleeing crowd, but he outran it. The four escaped without immediate identification.

The party learned later that nobody was killed. Several civilians briefly caught fire but suffered only superficial injury. Rise sent guards to find the names registered at the Hunt. The final extent of the barge damage was not observed, although the DM later described it as blown up and no longer usable for Vane's intended ferry crossing.

## Flight from Belhaven

Ordrin collected the wagon, two mules and adventuring supplies from the east gate and had stabled them at the Hunt. During the escape, Thud changed clothing, armour and weapons to alter his appearance. The party took all three contract sheets and chose the Quarry Breaker first.

They reached the road before realising they had no map. Lucian returned alone under his Sebastian identity, passed both the Hunt attendant and the heightened gate security, paid one gold from three supplied by the party and rejoined the others with the map. The party then travelled through the night, with Ordrin driving and the others able to take a short rest.

## The production guide

During the wagon journey, Nova found her bag unexpectedly empty while [[What the Fuck Is Going On]] appeared in Ordrin's hands. The principal cast recognised that it had not previously been there. According to the DM, non-principal observers would instead remember it as always having existed.

The book defines Stars as principal cast whose choices and failures contribute to the Show. It says Star Power measures Production's confidence that a character is recognisably themselves, rewarding consistency, style, development, relationships, commitment and entertainment value rather than mere success.

The book also describes Narrative Accommodation: Production may alter unresolved reality when an established Star makes a character-consistent assertion. If uncertain, the procedure is `d20 + floor(Star Power / 5)` against an undisclosed Production DC. Established canon and historical events cannot be changed. Deliberate attempts to manipulate the process are discouraged, and the book's warnings are incomplete.

Nova used Narrative Accommodation to establish that she knew the party's ratings:

| Character       | Star Power | Modifier |
| --------------- | ---------: | -------: |
| Lucian Vale     |         66 |      +13 |
| Ordrin Emberkeg |         64 |      +12 |
| Nova            |         60 |      +12 |
| Thud Firebelly  |         57 |      +11 |

The book says these values are recalculated at session end; the table records the values disclosed during play, before the Session 4 award.

Nova then stated that she had previously been principal cast, later failed and was recast as the toad Lucian compelled to stand between the party and the Fen Mother. She said that sacrificial moment allowed her to become a Star again and that she now carried both Nova's memories and the toad's memories. This is Nova's direct testimony within ordinary play. Its broader implications remain unresolved.

Ordrin gave the book to Nova for storage with the hammer.

## The Northern Quarry

At dawn the party reached [[Northern Quarry]] and met [[Foreman Brack]]. Workers had been driven from the upper workings by a four-legged charging beast with iron-like shoulders. Two retaining walls and three wagons had been destroyed and one worker remained badly bruised. Brack believed the creature had emerged from a fissure. A successful Nature check identified the likely quarry breaker as an ironbark charger, a heavily armoured beast sometimes used for siege work.

At the wrecked wagons, Thud confirmed one set of charger tracks descending from upslope, turning and returning upward. Nova took a payroll script and 30 gp from an unlocked pay strongbox. Ordrin found a coil of rope and a tin of lamp oil. The money was intended for quarry workers; no return was recorded.

At a mid-bench ledge, Nova spotted fist-sized rock-like insects below a failing crane. Thud tied a rope to the crane as a contingency, then threw an improvised fire bottle into two swarms of ten [[Bucket Scrabs]] each. Lucian used Faerie Fire, Ordrin used Sacred Flame, Nova's Thunderwave damaged both swarms and knocked them down the ledge, and Thud destroyed one swarm with his +1 maul.

Repeated attempts to pull the crane down failed until Thud crossed the battlefield, seized the rope and toppled the structure. The falling crane crushed the remaining swarm without blocking the path. A [[Surveyor's Compass]] glinted where the crane had stood.
