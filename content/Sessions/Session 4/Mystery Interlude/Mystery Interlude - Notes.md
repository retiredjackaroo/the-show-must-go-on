> This record is structurally separate from the ordinary Session 4 notes and narrative. Its host, set, audience, apparent rules and implications must not populate ordinary campaign characters, locations, factions, items or lore unless later play establishes them independently.

## Summary

Lucian, Ordrin and Thud experienced a second interview after a flash of light at the Open Bell sanctuary. The host's generated audio was not captured, so the questions and most reactions cannot be recovered reliably. The surviving player responses show that the interview focused on [[Vane's Warhammer]], its destruction of souls and memory, and whether the party should ever use it again. Ordrin's exclamation “what the fuck is going on?” coincided with the end of the event.

Nova did not participate. After the three returned, Nova asked whether they had discussed the phenomenon and whether they regarded it as a shared experience.

## Observed Sequence

1. The hammer was on the sanctuary floor when Lucian, Ordrin and Thud experienced a sudden flash and entered the interview.
2. The recording setup failed to capture most of the host's generated speech. Technical discussion about muting, push-to-talk and notification sounds occupies much of the surviving segment.
3. Thud answered one missing prompt with: “Why not? Hammer, hit, frog. It happens again.”
4. Ordrin asked the host to repeat a question.
5. Ordrin stated that death is natural but erasing someone from history is wrong.
6. Ordrin answered another missing prompt by saying the feared outcome was very unlikely.
7. Thud said he knew the hammer ate souls and erased memories and that it should be used occasionally.
8. Ordrin attempted to cast a spell, but the transcript rendered the words as “I cast tokens”; the exact spell is not recoverable from this recording alone.
9. Ordrin exclaimed, “what the fuck is going on?” The DM immediately said that he had “said the words,” and the event ended.
10. Nova then asked the returned trio about the phenomenon. Lucian said they could do little beyond experience it and expressed disappointment that he had not dominated the audience's attention this time.

## What Can and Cannot Be Established

- The participants were Lucian, Ordrin and Thud. Nova's questions occurred after their return.
- The event was an interview with the same or a similar host-and-audience framework as the earlier quarantined incident.
- The topic involved the hammer, forgotten victims, soul consumption and the possibility of further use.
- The players could answer the host.
- No complete host question, ruling, reward, punishment, score or new interaction rule survives in the recording.
- The transcript does not reliably establish visual details of the set, whether the host was physically vulnerable, or whether time passed outside the experience.
- The phrase that ended the event may have been a trigger, a coincidence or a production failure. The evidence does not decide between them.

## Return and Residue

- All three participants retained memories and treated the event as shared.
- Nova knew that a prior shared experience had occurred but was not part of this one.
- Lucian expected there might be another appearance and hoped to receive more attention next time.
- No magical investigation or physical residue was recorded after this second event.
