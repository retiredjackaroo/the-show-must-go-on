The hammer struck the sanctuary floor.

Light erased the church around Lucian, Ordrin and Thud, and the interview found them again.

This time, the record kept their answers and lost the questions.

Thud spoke first from the surviving fragments. Why not use the hammer again? Hit a frog. Let it happen.

Ordrin's answer came with more weight.

“Death is natural, but erasing someone from history, that's just wrong.”

Whatever the host asked next is gone. Ordrin believed the possibility was unlikely. Thud did not share his caution. He knew what the weapon did, he said. It ate souls and erased memories. They should use it occasionally.

The missing voice continued to press them. Ordrin tried to answer with magic. Even the name of the spell disappeared into the bad recording.

Then he demanded to know what was happening.

The words ended the interview.

The sanctuary returned. Nova had not gone with them. She asked the three whether they had spoken about these shared experiences and what they believed they were.

Lucian had no explanation. He knew only that there was little to do except endure them—and that, if there was a next time, he intended to claim more of the audience.

The questions remained unheard. The answers remained theirs.
