# Session 1: Notes

![[Assets/Greyfen.jpg]]

The party travelled through the marsh to [[Greyfen]], where Acting Administrator [[Maelin Voss]] had blocked a medicine barge while trying to contain an unidentified military runaway ahead of an inspection. The three forced the supplies to be unloaded, helped the clinic and followed [[Corrin Pike]] through the north marsh to reach the sick fugitive [[Tavia Rel]]. After escaping crocodiles, a corpse-light and the Fen Mother, they revealed their own military background to Tavia. She invited them into her hunting blind as dawn broke.

## Timeline

### 1. Bells on the road

- The party continued putting distance between itself and its former military camp.
- Greyfen's ferry was the known safe route across the surrounding swamp.
- Bell-like sounds in the fog proved to come from a rare local bell frog.
- Ordrin began a Detect Magic ritual; Thud failed to read the creature's intentions.
- Lucian cast Vicious Mockery and insulted the frog, revealing three creatures and beginning combat.

### 2. Bell-frog encounter

- Thud entered Rage and attacked with his hammer.
- Ordrin used Channel Divinity to add `+10` to Thud's missed attack, converting it into a hit for 14 damage.
- One frog grappled Thud; another later grappled Lucian.
- Lucian granted Combat Inspiration to Ordrin and Bardic Inspiration to Thud.
- Ordrin killed the frog holding Thud with a normal strike followed by a War Priest bonus attack.
- Ordrin critically missed while trying to free Lucian and dealt 2 damage to him instead.
- Lucian finished the final frog with his rapier.
- The party killed all three frogs and continued without a long rest.

### 3. Harvesting and recovery

- Ordrin recovered 2 sp worth of medicinal ingredients.
- Thud recovered 4 sp worth of edible or trade goods.
- The table established that harvested materials can be sold or spent toward crafting of the relevant type.
- Lucian cast Healing Word at 2nd level and expended his available 2nd-level slots.
- A large crocodile watched from nearby water but did not attack.
- The party left bridge-repair timber behind rather than carry dead weight.

### 4. Arrival in Greyfen

![[Assets/Greyfen Causeway.jpg]]

- Arrival was around 4 p.m.
- Greyfen is an abandoned military fort being repurposed as a settlement.
- A heavy chain blocked the ferry landing.
- A medicine barge waited offshore while guards held back angry residents.
- Residents believed Voss was trying to prevent someone escaping town.

### 5. Little Sarah and the outbreak

- [[Little Sarah]] collapsed coughing in the street.
- Her mother reported approximately 12 clinic patients and another 8–9 people developing symptoms.
- [[Brother Sen]] was said to be overwhelmed.
- Ordrin diagnosed a common, contagious and treatable nonmagical illness.
- Healing magic could relieve symptoms but not cure the underlying sickness.
- Ordrin cast Healing Word, temporarily restoring Sarah's strength.

### 6. First meeting with Maelin Voss

- The party entered the [[Old Standard Inn]], a former military mess hall.
- A dock worker coughed blood onto a table, demonstrating the outbreak's severity.
- [[Mara]] was identified as the bartender.
- Lucian adopted the alias **Sebastian Vale**, a wealthy merchant, with Thud and Ordrin as bodyguards.
- Voss said soldiers would arrive at midday for an “inspection” that concealed a search for a military runaway.
- She did not provide the fugitive's name or description.
- Voss offered 10 gp each if the party kept the inspection orderly and prevented the target being found.
- She accepted killing or capturing the fugitive as possible solutions.
- Lucian proposed that Voss unload the medicine while the party handled the fugitive.
- Voss issued the [[Badge of Authority]] and imposed the midday deadline.

### 7. Disguise, rest and wharf reconnaissance

- The party bathed, cleaned equipment and removed conspicuous military identifiers.
- Thud temporarily stashed his distinctive hammer and considered cutting hair with a dagger.
- They took a short rest, not a long rest.
- Lucian's intended cover was to guide the inspection as Sebastian Vale while Thud and Ordrin served as bodyguards.
- At the wharf, the head guard had received no order to unload the medicine.
- He also revealed the inspectors would enter through the main gate, not arrive by boat.
- This exposed Voss's failure to honour the bargain and undermined the party's first plan.

### 8. Second confrontation and unloading

![[Assets/Greyfen Wharf.jpg]]

- Voss attempted to redefine the agreement as requiring the party to bring her the fugitive.
- Lucian maintained that the agreement required only that the inspectors fail to find the person.
- Ordrin argued that withholding medicine made cooperation and trust impossible.
- Under combined persuasion and intimidation, Voss ordered the chain withdrawn and crates unloaded.
- She did **not** release the ferry boat; it remains held until after the inspection.
- Ordrin selected the supplies required to treat the illness.

### 9. Greyfen Clinic

![[Assets/Greyfen Clinic.jpg]]

- The party reached the clinic after midnight.
- Eleven patients occupied the old powder room; three were too ill to look toward the door.
- [[Answer Vale]], a retired field medic, immediately began using the supplies.
- Ordrin helped administer treatment.
- Thud guarded the entrance and kept his distance from the patients.
- Lucian remained outside because of his aversion to sickness.
- Answer revealed that [[Tavia Rel]] was sick and hiding in the north marsh.
- Tavia was described as early twenties, black-haired, cloaked and moving like someone with military training.
- She had arrived in Greyfen on the run and fled when rumours of the inspection began.
- Answer supplied a medical kit for reaching and treating her.

### 10. Hiring Corrin Pike

- [[Corrin Pike]] asked 1 gp to guide the party to Tavia.
- Corrin had seen two military forward scouts go the wrong way in the marsh.
- Ordrin's Insight indicated that Corrin was not leading them into an ambush or planning to sell them out.
- Corrin remained visibly worried and withheld some information.
- He could navigate in darkness without a normal light source; the reason is unresolved.

### 11. North-marsh approach

![[Assets/North Marsh.jpg]]

- Corrin warned of giant frogs, giant toads, crocodiles and the Fen Mother.
- The party saw giant-frog depressions and movement in the water.
- Torchlight improved footing but risked attracting bell frogs.
- Thud discarded the torch and the party followed Corrin in darkness.
- A blinking white light appeared behind them. Corrin did not recognise it.
- The group chose movement over confrontation and continued toward the blind.

### 12. Crocodiles, corpse-light and Fen Mother

![[Assets/Fen Mother Waters.jpg]]

![[Assets/Session 1 - Fen Mother Combat.png]]

- Lucian entered deep water and two crocodiles approached.
- Lucian cast Faerie Fire, outlining the crocodiles and unintentionally exposing the Fen Mother.
- Corrin panicked and attempted to flee; Thud grappled him to preserve the party's guide.
- Ordrin cast Toll the Dead against a crocodile, dealing 8 necrotic damage.
- Thud hit one crocodile for 7 damage; Lucian struck the other for 5 piercing damage.
- The blinking light was identified as a will-o'-wisp or “Corpse Light.”
- The will-o'-wisp attacked the Fen Mother, drawing the larger predator away from the party.
- The Fen Mother appeared effectively unharmed and destroyed the wisp with an 18-damage bite.
- The party disengaged rather than attempt to kill the Fen Mother.
- Lucian cast Command on a giant toad, ordering it to halt as a diversion. The DM ruled that this toad understood the command.
- Corrin led the party off the encounter map; no party member suffered serious damage in the escape.

### 13. Tavia Rel

![[Assets/North Marsh Hunting Blind.jpg]]

- The blind stood above a dark pool on bowing supports with a split ladder and oilcloth-covered window.
- Tavia held a light crossbow with correct form but lacked long-practised ease.
- Her trembling came from fever rather than fear.
- She wore military clothing beneath her cloak.
- The party did not personally recognise her, but her uniform matched their former service.
- Ordrin called out that they had medicine.
- Lucian revealed his military connection by playing a familiar company song in a recognisable voice.
- Tavia asked Corrin why he had brought them.
- Successful persuasion convinced her that the party was not there to capture her.
- She said, “Quickly, inside,” ending the session before her explanation.

## Character Contributions

### Lucian Vale

- Initiated the bell-frog encounter with Vicious Mockery.
- Used Bardic and Combat Inspiration during the fight.
- Acted as the party's principal negotiator with Voss.
- Built the Sebastian Vale merchant cover and used the authority badge at the wharf.
- Cast Faerie Fire during the crocodile encounter and Command during the escape.
- Used an old company song and his recognisable voice to gain Tavia's trust.

### Thud Firebelly

- Front-lined the bell-frog battle under Rage and absorbed a grappled bite.
- Harvested edible materials after combat.
- Added physical intimidation during the second confrontation with Voss.
- Discarded the torch when it threatened to attract more bell frogs.
- Grappled Corrin during his panic and kept him from abandoning the party.
- Recovered his shield and helped cover the retreat from the Fen Mother.

### Ordrin Emberkeg

- Used Channel Divinity and War Priest attacks decisively against the bell frogs.
- Diagnosed Little Sarah and temporarily relieved her symptoms.
- Kept the blocked medicine at the centre of both negotiations with Voss.
- Helped Answer Vale treat Greyfen's patients.
- Determined Corrin was broadly trustworthy despite withholding information.
- Used necrotic magic and tactical coordination during the marsh escape.
- Carried the medical purpose of the mission through to Tavia.

## NPC Intelligence

### Maelin Voss

- Wants the inspection to pass without a riot and its target to remain unfound.
- Values control and local stability, but accepted civilian suffering as the price of maintaining the closed wharf.
- Was willing to contemplate the fugitive's death.
- May know more than she disclosed; her description of the job changed under pressure.

### Answer Vale

- Retired field medic, direct and highly practical.
- Trusted the party with medicine and Tavia's location.
- Appears primarily motivated by patient welfare.

### Corrin Pike

- Competent marsh guide with unexplained night navigation.
- Knows Tavia and anticipated that she might shoot unfamiliar visitors.
- Knows two military scouts entered the marsh incorrectly.
- Honest about the route but concealing a concern or connection.

### Tavia Rel

- Young, sick, armed and military-trained or military-adjacent.
- On the run before reaching Greyfen.
- Spooked specifically by rumours of the inspection.
- Recognised the significance of Lucian's company song.
