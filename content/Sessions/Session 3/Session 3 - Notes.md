## Arrival and registration

Thud pushed Lucian off the crocodile skull and claimed the perch for the approach. When a missed dockline caused the salvage to shift and a brace to crack, Ordrin used Mending. [[Captain Oda Merrick]] brought the barge safely in.

[[Mara (Harbourmaster)|Mara]] took charge of the berth. Thud unloaded cargo; Ordrin enlisted a crane operator. Oda corroborated that the crocodile had attacked the vessel. The harbour hold was a safety measure, not a finding that the party caused the hull damage. Repair costs and responsibility remained unsettled.

Lucian persuaded Tavia and Marr to register under cover identities despite their concern that false names might weaken their evidence. No salvage claim was made in Belhaven. The sale of the party's existing Fen Mother loot materials was handled through [[Copperpot and Company]].

## The Hunt and the market fight

[[Pell]], an anatomy specialist from the [[Belhaven Hunt]], arranged appraisal and transport of the Fen Mother materials. He credited the decisive crossbow wound to Marr. Tavia and Marr went to the church for shelter while the three followed Pell.

A sedated adult [[Chainhide Prowler]] broke free after seeing a juvenile in a nearby cage. Thud tried unsuccessfully to break the juvenile's lock, hoping reunion would calm the adult. The juvenile then escaped by itself. A proposed attempt to restrain the adult using its chains was abandoned after the DM clarified that the chains were part of its body.

Ordrin fought in melee, suffered a knockdown and later used Guiding Bolt. Lucian outlined the adult with Faerie Fire and healed Ordrin. Nova fired a force ballista and radiant arrows from a rooftop. The adult broke open the bellfrog cage during the fight. Both prowlers ultimately died: Lucian killed the juvenile with Dissonant Whispers, much to Thud's displeasure. Ordrin's request to spare the adult did not change the later confirmed outcome.

Nova killed one bellfrog and Lucian finished another. Ordrin left the third unconscious. Thud pocketed one prowler scale as a personal trophy. Pell offered Hunt membership and accommodation but no bounty for animals that already belonged to someone else. The party used the Hunt's lodging and crafting contacts; individual membership paperwork was not detailed.

## Nova and an evening at the hall

Nova was recognised as a former company artillerist who had stayed loyal during the mutiny. Nova now sought answers and offered alliance, reporting that Vane had died before the search for the party began. Memories were hazy; the report did not resolve Vane's apparent survival. Lucian accepted the alliance cautiously, while Thud welcomed the help.

Ordrin's Augury reassured him that checking on the church in the morning could wait. Lucian performed his account of the Fen Mother battle, winning the hall's enthusiasm and spreading the party's reputation. The song's boasts remain Lucian's version of events.

## The hammer and the forgotten soldier

Thud could not remember Sebastian. Under Ordrin's Zone of Truth, his denial was sincere. Ordrin's deity condemned the forgetting of fallen soldiers; as Ordrin recounted Sebastian's life and death, the hammer grew hot. Detect Magic revealed a powerful item, and approaching it worsened Nova's memory fog.

Nova's Identify and the ensuing explanations established that a kill with the hammer erases memory of the victim and transfers soul power into the wielder, not into storage inside the weapon. Sufficient power can return the wielder to life one hour after death, with wounds knitting together; it cannot resurrect a chosen companion. The explanation offers a possible avenue for understanding Vane's survival, not proof of what happened to him.

The DM identified the weapon as an ancient necromantic artefact fashioned from the [[Beast of Forgotten Souls]], an elder beast. Its soul-consuming origin is canonical following the player's clarification, although its maker, age and original purpose remain unknown. Repeated use may have given Vane a similar resurrection power; this remains a possibility rather than a confirmed account of his survival.

Thud tested the weapon on the surviving unconscious bellfrog. The merchants, Lucian and Nova then perceived no frog and could not understand why he had struck the ground. Ordrin understood what had happened. Thud felt empowered; the DM quantified the gain as one sixteenth of the power needed for his own resurrection. He had hoped to save the juvenile prowler, whose body had already been processed, but learned that the power could only restore himself.

Ordrin demanded the hammer's destruction. Argument and intimidation failed. Lucian appealed to Thud's fear of forgetting his friends and ultimately used Friends; Thud placed the weapon into Nova's small extradimensional bag. Ordrin felt his deity's anger subside. The DM clarified that Thud's memory of Sebastian had returned through Ordrin's earlier remembrance, rather than first returning when the hammer was put away.

## The Open Bell

Karad Ren had sheltered Tavia and Marr overnight. In a private room, the party presented the ledger and accompanying papers, described falsified casualties and the disastrous mission, and offered testimony under Zone of Truth. Lucian succeeded on his save; the others' testimony must not be treated as proof that every statement by everyone was magically compelled.

Karad revealed that a representative of Vane already knew of the party and its documents. The military explanation was clerical error and misunderstood instructions, with corrected records and witnesses offered in support. Lucian refused the proposed military liaison's house as a meeting place. By session end, the invitation extended to a roof of their choosing in town and was described as non-combative. That is a promise, not a guarantee; no meeting took place.

After learning that the Open Bell's faith also abhorred the hammer's effects, the party described it and then revealed it. Thud placed it on the floor. Lucian claimed its fresh blood came from his injured foot; this was his explanation, not an established cause. They disclosed the memory effects but withheld their own killing of the bellfrog.

Karad committed to helping investigate the army and find a way to destroy the weapon. Lucian argued it should remain evidence until an authority could take the perpetrators into custody. The destruction method and arrest authority remained unresolved.

Lucian sang to memorialise Sebastian. People who knew him and heard the song remembered him, including his wife, who asked what had happened. Ordrin delivered the death notification and comforted her. At the docks, a dwarf working on the barge discovered Sebastian's body. Lucian promised his widow about 5 gp from the Fen Mother materials; payment was not recorded.
