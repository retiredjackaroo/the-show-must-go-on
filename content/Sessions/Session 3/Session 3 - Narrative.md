[[Belhaven]] saw the skull before it saw the heroes.

![[Assets/Session 3 - Belhaven Aerial.jpg|Belhaven on the eastern lakeshore, seen from above.]]

Work slowed along the quay as [[Captain Oda Merrick]] brought the damaged barge toward the stone berth. The Fen Mother's remains rode above a hull that still admitted lake water. Lucian had chosen the skull as a suitable platform for his arrival. Thud disagreed, and a determined shove left the bard on the deck while the barbarian enjoyed the view.

![[Assets/Session 3 - Belhaven Arrival.jpg|The damaged barge arriving at Belhaven with the Fen Mother's skull aboard.]]

The landing required more practical cooperation. A missed rope, a shift of cargo and a cracking brace threatened an ugly collision until Ordrin's Mending held the repair together. Then the harbourmaster arrived.

## Names for the ledger

[[Mara (Harbourmaster)|Mara]] looked past the monster and straight at the hole in the barge. She took charge of the berth, ordered the cargo clear and made it plain that the vessel would remain under inspection until it was safe. Thud protected his prize by helping unload it. Ordrin found a crane operator. Lucian found the paperwork.

Tavia and Marr wanted their own names attached to the evidence they carried. Lucian wanted control over who could find them. His argument prevailed. [[Copperpot and Company]] entered Belhaven with a brewer at its head, a bookkeeper, a driver, two porters and an antiques broker. The names in the register were borrowed. The crocodile was very real.

It brought them [[Pell]], a Hunt specialist whose curiosity ran to preserved glands, measurable wounds and the exact angle of the fatal crossbow bolt. Tavia and Marr left to seek church shelter; the others followed the Fen Mother materials uphill toward the [[Belhaven Hunt]], where their sale would be handled through Copperpot and Company.

## The cages open

The market outside the hall displayed creatures as curiosities, merchandise and future materials. A small six-legged prowler watched from one cage. A much larger one arrived sedated on a cart.

![[Assets/Session 3 - Belhaven Market.jpg|Belhaven's crowded market gathered around the Fen Mother's skull.]]

Then the adult saw the juvenile.

![[Assets/Session 3 - Adult Chainhide Prowler.jpg|The adult chainhide prowler breaking free of its restraint.]]

![[Assets/Session 3 - Juvenile Chainhide Prowler.jpg|The bronze-scaled juvenile chainhide prowler.]]

Its containment failed. Civilians scattered. Thud tried to free the smaller animal, hoping that bringing them together would end the panic. His hammer failed against the lock, and the adult saw a threat where he had meant kindness. When the juvenile escaped on its own, the fighting was already under way.

Ordrin met the adult at close quarters and was knocked to the ground. Lucian's Faerie Fire outlined its armoured body, giving his companions clearer targets, and his healing kept Ordrin in the fight. From a rooftop came another answer: a lance of force, then a radiant arrow.

[[Nova]] had found them.

The adult crashed through a bellfrog cage before the fight ended. Ordrin tried to spare creatures where he could, but both prowlers died. Lucian's Dissonant Whispers killed the juvenile; his satisfied announcement landed badly with Thud. Two frogs died in the fighting, while another lay unconscious. Thud took a single scale as a keepsake.

Pell returned with reinforcements to find that the emergency was over. There would be no hunting bounty for someone else's stock, but there was a place for capable fighters at the hall.

## An old comrade, an absent friend

Nova was familiar: an artillerist from the company, once loyal to Vane when the others mutinied. Now that loyalty had changed. Nova wanted answers, remembered Vane dying, and had followed the party's trail through Greyfen to Belhaven. Some memories were becoming difficult to hold.

Lucian's welcome came with questions. Thud's came with praise for the shooting and a preference for continuing the discussion over a drink.

Inside, the Hunt offered beds, food, crafting contacts and stories of other quarry. Thud favoured the plated beast troubling the northern quarry road. Ordrin sought reassurance through Augury and received a calming answer: their church business could wait until morning. Lucian gave the hall a song about the Fen Mother, and the audience gave him its enthusiasm.

![[Assets/Session 3 - Belhaven Hunt Hall.jpg|The trophy-filled common hall of the Belhaven Hunt.]]

Then the conversation turned to Sebastian, and Thud did not know whom they meant.

Not a forgotten detail. Not an uncertain face. As far as he knew, there had been no Sebastian.

Ordrin called on truth, and Thud's answer remained sincere. His god's anger followed. A soldier who had chosen to fight and fallen should be remembered. As Ordrin recounted Sebastian's part in their escape and his death aboard the ferry, [[Vane's Warhammer]] grew hot against Thud's back.

## What the weapon keeps

Thud dropped the hammer onto the table. Ordrin sensed its power; Nova leaned closer and felt the fog in memory thicken. Curiosity nevertheless won, and Nova examined it with Identify.

The explanation was worse than a curse on a weapon. It was an ancient necromantic artefact fashioned from the [[Beast of Forgotten Souls]], an elder creature whose nature survived in the hammer's work. Those killed with it were forgotten. Their souls strengthened the wielder, who could spend that accumulated power to return from death.

Ordrin wanted it destroyed. Thud wanted to test it.

Outside, the unconscious frog was still alive. One blow changed that. The people handling it suddenly saw only a man striking the ground. Lucian and Nova could no longer make sense of the act either. Ordrin could.

Thud felt stronger. He went looking for the dead juvenile, hoping that the new power might save it, and found only a body already stripped for materials. The hammer offered no such kindness. It could restore its wielder, not another creature.

The argument that followed exhausted Ordrin's patience and Thud's willingness to be told what to do. A promised replacement weapon offered a practical way forward, but persuasion alone did not settle it. Lucian used Friends, and Thud finally placed the great hammer into Nova's surprisingly small bag. It disappeared inside. Ordrin felt the divine anger ease.

Sebastian's memory had returned to Thud through Ordrin's telling. Putting the hammer away helped contain the immediate problem, but it did not answer every question.

## A better hammer

The Hunt could turn the Fen Mother's skull into [[Grandmother's Grasp]], a magical maul whose jaws could clamp an enemy, bring it down and drag it. Thud paid the additional hundred gold. The whole skull went into the commission, with completion promised after two more sessions.

He would not spend those sessions unarmed. The party sold other crocodile materials to buy a +1 maul. Lucian arranged an armour upgrade with Ordrin's help. Then they rested and went to find their witnesses.

## The bell and the song

The [[Belhaven Open Bell Sanctuary]] stood above the harbour with open doors, crowded benches and brass nameplates catching the light. [[Karad Ren]], a lay brother with ink-stained fingers, had cared for Tavia and Marr overnight.

![[Assets/Belhaven Open Bell Sanctuary.jpg|The Open Bell sanctuary overlooking Belhaven and the lake.]]

In a private room, the party laid out its evidence. False casualty figures. Diverted supplies. Orders that should not have been given. Karad heard them under Zone of Truth, but sincerity alone did not settle what a powerful military command could dispute.

Vane's representative had reached him first.

The explanation offered was error: bad clerical work, misunderstood instructions, corrected numbers and witnesses ready to support them. There was also an invitation to meet. Lucian refused military premises. If there was to be a conversation, the party would choose the roof.

The hammer changed Karad's response. Ordrin described its effects and his god's revulsion. Thud took it from the bag and set it on the floor. Karad asked about the fresh blood; Lucian offered a story about his foot. The party's own experiment remained unspoken.

Even so, Karad saw enough to commit himself. The weapon had to be destroyed, and the army had to be investigated. Lucian wanted it kept as evidence until someone could act against those responsible. The means remained uncertain, but they had an ally.

To explain the man they had lost, Lucian sang.

People who had known Sebastian remembered him. A woman came forward, suddenly able to name her husband and unable to understand how she had forgotten him. Lucian turned to Ordrin, who gave the news with the solemnity it deserved and stayed to comfort her.

From the docks came a scream. Someone working on the damaged barge had found the body.

The supply run was still broken. Vane's invitation still waited. The hammer lay before them, and its victims could not yet all be named. But Sebastian had a wife who remembered him, a cleric to mourn him and a song that had brought him back into the world of the living, if only as a memory.
