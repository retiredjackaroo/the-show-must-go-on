Belhaven wanted four fugitives.

Lucian entered alone.

He wore Sebastian's face, passed the gate under lockdown and walked through a town trying to live around the wound the party had left at its harbour. The ferry was gone. Little boats and skiffs had taken its work. Greyfen's supplies were moving again. Vane was still expected. The guards were still looking for Lucian, Nova, Thud and Ordrin.

![[Assets/Session 6 - Belhaven Broadsheet.jpg|Belhaven under the shadow of the ferry attack.]]

At the [[Belhaven Hunt]], Sebastian sold Ordrin's chainmail. He hired a field dresser named Jason, bought javelins, herbs and food, then did what Lucian Vale had always done best when caution would have been wiser.

He took the stage.

The Catastrophe song finally found its audience. Then Lucian played something new.

![[Assets/Session 6 - Ordrin Memorial Performance.jpg|Lucian performs Ordrin's memorial at the Hunt.]]

[[Ordrin Did It]] began with the weapons and ale. It remembered Ordrin patching Thud together. It remembered the bell-frog and the arrow in Lucian's foot. It remembered the cleric who would argue with a captain, a host or a god.

Then it blamed him for the barge.

The room laughed until it understood that Ordrin was dead.

The whispers changed. A few people cried. Others left quickly. Lucian stood in the silence his own song had made and raised his voice.

“Ordrin Emberkeg died as he lived,” he said. “On fire and slightly annoying.”

Then Sebastian collected Jason and left town before anyone decided to ask a second question.

## The hill that cried

The road east lasted two days and then surrendered.

Beyond it lay a valley that should have smelled of wool and hearth smoke. Instead there was snow, iron and an unnatural cry sliding down the mountainsides. Meridian Hill rose through the storm like a black tooth. On its crown, an abandoned observatory showed a broken ring, iced brass and a dome open to the sky.

At the shepherd cairn, knife-cut warnings marked wings, paws and insects. At the trail camp, a shepherd named Kelm was gathering the last of his flock. One sheep was missing. So was Rusk, his dog.

Another man waited there: Juno of the Instrument Guild, brave enough to want the observatory's treasures and sensible enough not to fetch them himself. He offered rewards for a meridian-arm blueprint, journals and charts, the fate of a missing Hunt expedition and one deactivated construct guard.

The party lit the campfire anyway.

The first two [[Chart Rider|Chart Riders]] came out of the dark like enormous eagles. Nova's opening arrows vanished into the low light. Ember stepped out of her bedroll, raised one hand and erased both birds with a Fireball.

Then the rest came.

Lucian put out the real fire and placed an illusory one behind the party. The predators changed course and flew through the defenders toward a flame that could not warm them. Nova's radiant arrows struck through the dark. Ember filled the sky with rays and fire. Thud stood between the flock and everyone else.

For the first time, he invoked the jaws of [[Grandmother's Grasp]]. They closed around a Chart Rider and killed it. The carcass stayed trapped in the maul, so Thud beat another bird with its dead companion.

Eleven fell. One escaped.

By morning, the trail camp belonged to the party.

## Teeth in the snow

Kelm remembered the tag on Rusk's collar. The [[Surveyor's Compass]] remembered it too.

Its second hand pointed toward Windcut Ridge.

The party found great pawprints and the torn body of a hill ram. Thud, somehow, was the only one quiet enough to avoid announcing his approach. The howls began anyway.

Two [[Rime Stalker|Rime Stalkers]] came out of the snow.

Nova's first arrow struck one. Ember burned it. Then the other crossed the ground in a rush, knocked Nova down and opened its jaws. Its breath rolled over Nova and Lucian in a cone of killing cold.

Nova stopped moving.

Lucian barely remained standing.

![[Assets/Session 6 - Rime Stalker Fight.jpg|Thud charges a Rime Stalker on Windcut Ridge.]]

Thud ran thirty-five feet in a straight line and hit the second wolf with everything he had.

Grandmother's Grasp bit first. The ivory trophy from the Ironbark Rammer answered next. It awakened in the blow as the [[Horn of Ramming Speed]], drove the beast fifteen feet through the snow and carried Thud after it. He hit again and left it clinging to life.

Lucian put an arrow into the other wolf, then spent his strongest Healing Word on Nova. She woke with the creature still over her, stood and refused to retreat.

Ember solved the problem by becoming the worst thing on the ridge.

Fear rolled from her in a cone. Both wolves broke. So did Nova, who fled from Ember with them. One Rime Stalker passed Thud and died under his hammer. The other ran west while Thud chased it, furious that his salvage was getting away.

Nova recovered first. From hundreds of feet downrange, she put a radiant arrow into the fleeing shape.

The wolf reached Echo Gap alive.

The mosquitoes were waiting.

## The echo gap

The [[Gap Siphon|Gap Siphons]] swarmed the frightened wolf and stripped away what the party had not. Then they turned on the people following it.

Lucian caught almost the entire colony in Hypnotic Pattern. The giant mosquitoes hung helpless in the air. The party could have crossed the bridge and left them there.

Ember hated bugs.

![[Assets/Session 6 - Echo Gap Fight.jpg|Fire consumes the Gap Siphons above the Echo Gap bridge.]]

Her Fireball burst through the swarm, killing four and waking the survivors. Lucian, already wounded, watched his careful solution become a cloud of angry burning insects.

Thud ran onto the iced brass bridge.

Lucian and Nova had crossed it by falling, striking the metal like a gong and sliding. Thud rolled onto the hazard as if born there. He surfed the length of the bridge, jumped Lucian at the far end, landed in a heroic pose and looked back as though it had been easy.

Lucian marked the swarm with Faerie Fire. Nova's Scorching Rays killed two. Ember burned another. Thud waited for the rest.

One Siphon charged and buried its feeding head in his chest. Thud ignored it long enough to crush another, then seized the attached creature by the wings and pulled. The body came away. The head did not.

When the fighting ended, Nova heard a sheep beyond the bridge.

Thud, still carrying part of a giant mosquito in his chest, persuaded the animal to follow him. They put it into Nova's extradimensional bag just long enough to cross the bridge, returned it to Kelm alive and collected a reward wildly out of proportion to the sheep.

Rusk was still missing.

## Under the ice

While the party rested, a war horn sounded from the summit.

It was not the Cry.

The whole mountain answered by becoming silent. The wind stopped. The blizzard stopped. The Cry vanished.

The party went to the Glacier Tongue.

Four feet of clear ice covered fast black water. Two feet down, something delicate shone like a dream catcher. Ember flew above it and began cutting a tunnel with Fire Bolt. When she anchored herself with pitons and leaned down to work, the glacier cracked.

![[Assets/Session 6 - Glacier Tongue.jpg|The attempt to recover the Echo Catcher from the glacier.]]

A slab fifteen feet across broke free beneath her. Twenty-five tons of ice rolled in the current.

Nova skated close enough to cast Identify. The object was an [[Ear-Notch Echo Catcher]], a rare charm that could tell truth from echo, silence metal and steady a frightened mind.

Now everyone wanted it.

Ropes went out. Fire widened the shaft. Nova shattered the slab. The upper ice broke away, the rope tore loose and the catcher appeared for one moment, still frozen into the rolling block.

Ember landed on it.

Twice she tried to pull the charm free. Twice her strength failed. The slab turned and slipped beneath the surrounding ice. One final Fire Bolt struck through the water and released the catcher.

The current took it.

Ember prepared to give herself gills and follow. The water would not have cared. It moved too quickly, beneath too much ice, toward a destination no one knew. She looked once more into the dark and stepped back.

The party had reached Meridian Hill. They had killed what hunted the camp, survived the ridge, crossed the Echo Gap and rescued the sheep. Above them waited the observatory, the missing dog and whatever had silenced the storm.

Below them, the Echo Catcher kept moving.
