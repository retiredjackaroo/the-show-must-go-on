Lucian Vale, Thud Firebelly, Nova and Ember left the [[Northern Quarry]], passed through a locked-down [[Belhaven]] and began the [[Belhaven Hunt]]'s Eastern Cry contract at [[Meridian Hill]]. Lucian hired a field-dressing specialist, performed [[Ordrin Did It]] at the Hunt and publicly announced Ordrin's death. On the mountain the party defended a shepherd camp from twelve [[Chart Rider|Chart Riders]], followed the [[Surveyor's Compass]] toward a missing sheepdog, fought two [[Rime Stalker|Rime Stalkers]], cleared ten [[Gap Siphon|Gap Siphons]] from the Echo Gap and recovered a lost sheep. At the Glacier Tongue they identified but failed to recover the [[Ear-Notch Echo Catcher]], which was swept beneath the ice.

## Belhaven and Ordrin's memorial

![[Assets/Session 6 - Belhaven Broadsheet.jpg|A supplied broadsheet illustration for Belhaven's response to the ferry attack. Its artwork does not establish facts beyond recorded play.]]

Four days after the dock explosion, Belhaven was operating under heightened security. The ferry had been replaced by small boats and skiffs supplying Greyfen. Town talk still expected Lord-Captain Vane, and guards were looking for Lucian, Nova, Thud and Ordrin under their registered identities.

Lucian entered as Sebastian. At the Hunt he sold Ordrin's chainmail for the table-recorded 35 gp, hired Jason, bought five javelins for Thud, 10 gp of herbs for Ember and ten rations, then performed his Catastrophe song successfully enough to raise its popularity by one.

![[Assets/Session 6 - Ordrin Memorial Performance.jpg|Lucian performs Ordrin's memorial song at the Belhaven Hunt.]]

Lucian then performed [[Ordrin Did It]]. His Performance result of 18 made it a crowd favourite and increased its popularity by two. The song revealed Ordrin's death and named him as the barge bomber. Lucian followed it by announcing that Ordrin “died as he lived, on fire and slightly annoying.” The crowd mourned, whispered about the supposed bomber and sent several listeners away, possibly to alert Lucan Rhyse. The song establishes what Lucian publicly claimed, not the truth of responsibility for the explosion.

## Road to the Eastern Cry

During two days of travel, Thud and Lucian tried to teach Jason to use Ordrin's shield. Ember gathered 14 gp of potion herbs and completed a Potion of Healing using 25 gp of materials. Ember, Thud and Nova hunted four rations of rabbit.

At the base of [[Meridian Hill]], the old pasture was buried under a weeks-long unnatural blizzard. A brass-edged cry moved through the valley from the abandoned observatory on the crown. The shepherd cairn warned of wings, paws and insects: Chart Riders, Rime Stalkers and Gap Siphons.

Kelm was bringing down his last sheep. One ewe and his dog Rusk were missing. He said the Cry and blizzards began roughly six weeks earlier and described an unknown creature with a huge wingspan nesting near the observatory. At the trail camp, Juno of the Instrument Guild offered magical items and gold for the observatory's meridian-arm blueprint, journals and charts, the fate of an earlier Hunt group and one deactivated construct guard.

## Chart Rider attack

The party camped overnight despite knowing the fire would attract Chart Riders. Ember destroyed the first two with Fireball. More arrived in waves until twelve had attacked.

1. Lucian's Hypnotic Pattern brought one bird down, but its fall ended the effect; Dissonant Whispers later drove that bird from the fight.
2. Nova struck the airborne creatures with radiant True Strike arrows. Ember used another Fireball, Scorching Ray and a close electrical attack.
3. Thud protected Ember and Nova, killed birds with [[Grandmother's Grasp]] and invoked Death Roll for the first time. One carcass remained trapped in the maul's jaws and became an improvised weapon against another bird.
4. Lucian smothered the real campfire and placed an illusory replacement behind the party, redirecting the later wave through the defenders.
5. Ember's final Fireball killed two more. The party killed eleven Chart Riders; one fled.

Kelm rewarded the defence with two Potions of Healing, four cold-weather outfits, four climber's kits, four healer's kits, 100 gp of smoked mutton, 40 gp of wool, a 150 gp silver pay tin and the [[Caretaker's Storm Lantern]]. Jason's field dressing recovered feathers worth 110 gp and talons worth 220 gp.

## Windcut Ridge and the Rime Stalkers

After the long rest, Kelm concentrated on Rusk's tag and used the [[Surveyor's Compass]] to point the party north toward Windcut Ridge. The trail held large pawprints and a mutilated hill ram. The party tried to move quietly; only Thud passed the stealth test, and two Rime Stalkers attacked.

![[Assets/Session 6 - Rime Stalker Fight.jpg|Thud engages a Rime Stalker while the party fights across the frozen ridge.]]

1. Nova and Ember opened at range. A Rime Stalker then knocked Nova prone, bit her and struck Nova and Lucian with a cone of cold. Nova fell unconscious; Lucian was reduced to 2 HP after the second creature attacked him.
2. Thud raged and charged 35 feet into the second Rime Stalker. Grandmother's Grasp dealt 25 damage before the Ironbark horn awakened as the [[Horn of Ramming Speed]], adding 9 damage and driving the beast 15 feet back. Thud's second strike left it barely alive.
3. Lucian hit the other wolf with his shortbow and revived Nova with a third-level Healing Word for 17 HP. Nova stood and held the line with Dodge.
4. Ember cast Fear on both wolves and accidentally caught Nova in the cone. One Rime Stalker fled past Thud and died to his opportunity attack. Nova escaped the fear after dashing down-trail.
5. The remaining wolf fled toward Echo Gap while the party pursued. Nova struck it from extreme longbow range. At the gap, the frightened wolf ran through a colony of Gap Siphons and was killed by their attacks.

The two Rime Stalkers yielded two lots of pelts and fangs valued at 260 gp each.

## Echo Gap

The Echo Gap was crossed by an icy brass bridge above the glacier's source. Lucian tried to neutralise the ten Gap Siphons with Hypnotic Pattern, leaving all but one incapacitated. Ember attacked the clustered insects with Fireball anyway, killing four and breaking the spell on the survivors.

![[Assets/Session 6 - Echo Gap Fight.jpg|Ember's fire engulfs the Gap Siphons at the bridge.]]

Thud crossed the hazardous bridge with a natural 20, sliding its length and vaulting Lucian before readying his hammer. Lucian marked the surviving insects with Faerie Fire. Nova killed two with Scorching Ray, Ember killed another with Fire Bolt and Thud crushed those that reached him. One Gap Siphon embedded its feeding head in Thud's chest; he tore away its wings and body, leaving the head stuck until the fight was over.

Jason assisted with salvage. Ten Gap Siphon heads were valued at 120 gp. Nova heard the missing sheep beyond the bridge, and Thud won its trust with Animal Handling, help and Bardic Inspiration. The party placed it briefly in Nova's extradimensional bag to cross the bridge and returned it alive before its air ran out. Kelm rewarded the rescue with a Potion of Cold Resistance, a Potion of Greater Healing, four sets of pitons and 250 gp.

## Glacier Tongue and the lost Echo Catcher

During a short rest, a war-horn sound came from the summit. The Cry, wind and blizzard immediately stopped. The party nevertheless continued to the Glacier Tongue, where the compass still pointed east toward Rusk.

![[Assets/Session 6 - Glacier Tongue.jpg|Ember attempts to recover the Echo Catcher from the unstable glacier ice.]]

Nova spotted a delicate object two feet down in four-foot-thick clear ice above fast meltwater. Ember used flight and repeated Fire Bolts to melt toward it, then anchored a climbing kit into the weakened ice. A 15-by-15-foot slab broke free and began rolling in the current. Nova approached closely enough to cast Identify and learned that the object was the rare [[Ear-Notch Echo Catcher]].

The group tried ropes, further melting and Shatter. The slab split, but the rope tore loose and the item remained embedded. Ember landed on the unstable block and twice failed to pull it free. A final underwater Fire Bolt dislodged the catcher, but it floated beneath the surrounding ice and out of reach. Ember considered entering the current with Alter Self, then withdrew after the DM established that the flow would carry and drown her despite water breathing.
