The first thing Lucian and Thud did when Ember appeared above the broken crane was shoot her.

Both arrows found their mark. Both glanced away from the small golden scales across her chest. Ember looked down from the ledge in wild hair and rough hides and called them rude.

Thud saw a stranger in a dangerous quarry and trusted the warning in his gut. He charged the slope, lost his footing and fell back eight feet without injury to anything but his dignity. When he climbed again, more carefully, Ember finally gave them a name.

She was human, a sorcerer, and carried draconic magic in her blood.

She had come because dragons were watching [[Vane's Warhammer]]. According to Ember, Vexilfrax had once shared his territory with other dragons, then changed. He broke their lairs, scattered their families and drove the survivors away. Those exiles had spent centuries studying ruins and failed expeditions for anything that might threaten him.

The hammer had caught their attention. They believed it had been made from an elder beast that no longer existed. They had watched Vane draw power through it. If one elder creature could be unmade or changed, perhaps another could be too.

Ember wanted answers. The party wanted the quarry contract. For the moment, those paths ran together.

## Over the edge

The retaining wall ended at a twenty-foot drop. Thud went first.

Halfway down the rope, he caught the sharp smell of explosives inside an old blast hole. A half-full tin of blasting powder came loose reluctantly, slipped from his hand and nearly fell before he caught it against the wall.

“Look at me,” he shouted up the cliff. “I've got more explosives.”

The quarry answered with rustling from both sides.

Two dog-sized [[Rust Gnawer|automatons]] came along the wall. They were quarry tools turned hostile: iron teeth, armoured bodies and magnetic workings that pulled at Thud's shield. He climbed back up, drew his bow and missed. Then he met the first machine with warhammer and shield.

![[Assets/Session 5 - Rust Gnawer.jpg|One of the Rust Gnawers at the retaining breach.]]

The construct poisoned him. Its partner tore through the retaining wall. Nova's arrow missed its target and buried itself in Thud's shoulder, but she recovered and kept sending radiant shots into the machines. Ember's fire struck their rusted plates. Lucian vanished, left an illusion at the edge, healed the barbarian from nowhere and fed him the inspiration to keep swinging.

Thud kept fighting.

His hammer caught the first machine and drove it backward. It disappeared over the lip and struck the shelf below. The second locked itself magnetically to his shield. Thud dropped the shield, took the hammer in both hands and sent that one over too.

Now the fight was twenty feet beneath him.

Thud found a rock close to five hundred pounds and pushed it over the edge. It landed for thirty-nine points of damage. He followed it with another stone. Above him, Ember, Nova and Lucian turned the ledge into a firing line. Fire, radiant arrows and cutting magic rained down until the last construct leaked burning oil and struggled to move.

Then Thud jumped.

He hit the shelf badly, recovered, and swung the maul through the construct's head. Metal fragments struck the quarry wall like shot.

The way down was open.

## Building the battlefield

Three quarry workers had survived on the lower shelf. They had watched the quarry breaker investigate the party's noise, destroy a mule and cart and withdraw toward its cave. The creature was fast, heavily armoured at the front and strong enough to turn a charge into demolition.

Thud did not want to fight it in its lair. He wanted it to fight in his.

The plan was Thud's, but the battlefield belonged to all four of them. Two lines of hunting traps crossed the narrow approach, their plates disguised with broken stone and carpentry work. The recovered blasting powder waited beside the route. Ropes offered ways onto the higher ledges. Lucian prepared an illusion to stand where a target ought to be. Nova selected lines for her bow and force ballista. Ember took her place with fire and draconic terror ready.

When everything was ready, Thud struck a quarry cart like a gong.

The [[Ironbark Rammer]] came out of the cave hard enough to shake the ground.

![[Assets/Session 5 - Ironbark Rammer.jpg|The Ironbark Rammer charges from its cave.]]

Nova's first arrow glanced from the frontal armour, but her force ballista struck. Lucian's false Thud held the centre of the trap line. The Rammer charged the illusion and stepped into the first trap. Steel jaws closed and stopped it short.

Thud threw a burning bottle at the blasting powder. The bottle went wide. He drew on every reserve he had, threw again and turned the centre of the ambush into fire and rubble.

The Rammer survived.

It broke the first trap and entered the second. Lucian painted it with Faerie Fire, turning its weak points into targets for the whole party. Thud crossed the broken ground, came in behind its armoured head and drove the maul into the unprotected rear-left knee. Nova's radiant arrow struck the same weakness.

The Rammer turned on Thud.

It could have broken free. Instead it drove both horns at him. Rage took the worst from the blows. Thud stayed on his feet, tried to throw a lasso around its neck, thought better of spending the opening and hammered the knee again.

Ember reached into the draconic fear inside her and made the quarry breaker hesitate. Fire followed. With Lucian's magic exposing the target and Thud holding its fury at the wall, Nova found the rear angle and finished the beast with an arrow and a force-ballista strike.

It died among shattered traps, burnt stone and ropes: a kill assembled from Lucian's deception, Ember's fear, Nova's precision and Thud's gloriously excessive battlefield engineering. Thud stood against the wall before it, bruised, scorched and plainly having the time of his life.

## The rest of the quarry

Twenty ironbark plates came off the carcass. Thud and Ember each took an ivory horn nub. The contract was done, but Thud had heard there was a very large snake in the eastern gallery.

He went looking for a trophy.

Ember's light found the creature coiled in the timber. Nova's force ballista tore into its face before it could strike. Lucian's magic helped control the gallery while Thud raged, ran forward and swung twice. The snake never took a turn. He left with its tongue.

The collapsed tunnel beyond was less cooperative. Two [[Seam Lurker|Seam Lurkers]] moved under the rubble and surfaced where the stone was weakest. Lucian engaged one when it emerged. Nova waited for a clear point-blank shot, and Ember's draconic fear drove at least one creature back. At first Thud sat above the fighting with a drink and negotiated with Lucian over how many verses his victories deserved.

![[Assets/Session 5 - Seam Lurker.jpg|A Seam Lurker erupts from the rubble.]]

Then the creatures disappeared underground.

Thud slid from the rock, stepped in front of Lucian and raised the maul.

One lurker erupted beside him. The prepared blow landed before its claws did. It still raked poison across him, but on his next turn Thud crushed it and ran toward the others. His second strike found only stone. Ember's fear had already disrupted the creatures, Lucian had fought them at close range, and Nova's point-blank arrow killed the remaining one.

The party searched the eastern workings, bottled a sample from an iron seep and found a failed hunters' cache in the Rammer's lair. By the time they left, they carried twenty-two ironbark plates, construct scrap, drill fittings, hides, teeth, claws and several increasingly personal trophies.

They returned to the hoist camp around midday.

A courier was waiting for Thud.

[[Grandmother's Grasp]], the maul commissioned from the Fen Mother's skull, had arrived.

So had a broadsheet.

The four hunters had gained seven thousand experience each. They were level six. Lucian had made enemies fight ghosts and lit the party's targets. Ember had walked into a hail of arrows, joined the company anyway and brought dragonfire into the quarry. Nova had delivered the Rammer's final wounds and the last shot of the expedition.

Thud had turned a cliff, two traps, half a tin of blasting powder, several unreasonable rocks and one reckless jump into the finest day he had enjoyed in some time. The Quarry Breaker was dead, the quarry was open, and the session's happiest and most valuable barbarian had a new hammer.
