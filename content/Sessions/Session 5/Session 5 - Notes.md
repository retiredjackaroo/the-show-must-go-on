At the [[Northern Quarry]], Ember joined Lucian Vale, Thud Firebelly and Nova. She is a human sorcerer whose magic comes from Draconic Sorcery. She said she had been sent by exiled dragons to investigate [[Vane's Warhammer]], its elder-beast origin and Vane's use of it. The four descended through the quarry, defeated two [[Rust Gnawer|Rust Gnawers]], killed the [[Ironbark Rammer]] and cleared an enormous snake and two [[Seam Lurker|Seam Lurkers]] from the eastern workings. Thud led the quarry fighting: he turned both rust constructs over the retaining edge, built the Rammer ambush, detonated recovered blasting powder, held the Rammer in melee and killed the snake in a single turn. The party completed the Quarry Breaker hunt, reached level 6 and returned to the hoist camp, where [[Grandmother's Grasp]] was delivered.

## Ember

Ember appeared above the broken crane dressed in crude hide clothing, with wild hair and small golden scales across her chest and torso. Lucian and Thud loosed arrows at her before anyone spoke; both arrows glanced from the scales. Thud tried to rush up the ledge, slipped back down and then approached more carefully. Ember introduced herself only after the group stopped treating her as quarry.

The following are Ember's claims, not independently verified setting facts:

- She was raised far from the quarry by her mother, whom she identifies as a dragon.
- Vexilfrax once tolerated other dragons within his territory, then destroyed their lairs, scattered the survivors and drove them into exile for reasons they do not understand.
- The region contains ruins of dragon lairs and of armies or expeditions that tried to kill Vexilfrax.
- The exiled dragons have scryed [[Vane's Warhammer]] and watched Vane absorb power through it.
- They believe the hammer was made from the essence of an elder beast she called “the Unmade,” and regard its apparent destruction or alteration as evidence that Vexilfrax might also be defeated or changed.
- Ember came to learn what happened to the elder beast, how the hammer works and how Vane is involved. She expects to return that information to the exiled dragons.

The name “Vexilfrax” is the clearest transcript spelling but was queried aloud and should remain provisional until confirmed. “The Unmade” may conflict with the established [[Beast of Forgotten Souls]] name or may be another title; do not merge or replace the canonical elder-beast record without clarification.

Observed magic included Friends, Fire Bolt, Feather Fall, a fear-inducing draconic ability, Innate Sorcery and controlled aerial movement. Ember used a ring to make a small flame for light. Exact sheet mechanics remain unreconciled.

## Surveyor's Compass clarification

The opening recap clarified that the [[Surveyor's Compass]] has a true-north hand and a second hand that can point toward a previously seen object within range. The table described this as casting Locate Object once per day without requiring the bearer to maintain concentration. Final custody still was not stated.

## Retaining breach and the Rust Gnawers

![[Assets/Session 5 - Rust Gnawer.jpg|A Rust Gnawer in the ruined quarry workings.]]

At the retaining breach, the only route down was a 20-foot rope descent. Thud checked the landing first. Halfway down, he smelled unspent explosives inside a blast hole, pulled loose a half-full tin of blasting powder and nearly dropped it before catching it. He then shouted his discovery loudly enough to draw two dog-sized quarry automatons.

### Combat record

1. Thud climbed back to the upper ledge and opened with his longbow, then changed to warhammer and shield. He used Reckless Attack without raging and missed both initial swings.
2. Ember hit one construct with Fire Bolt. Nova damaged it with a radiant True Strike arrow. Lucian placed an illusory copy of himself at the cliff edge and made himself invisible.
3. One construct broke a retaining-wall section; the other engaged Thud, magnetised his shield, poisoned him and dealt heavy damage. Nova's failed shot struck Thud for 6 damage. Lucian healed him and supplied Bardic Inspiration.
4. Thud struck the first construct with the warhammer's pushing technique and drove it over the 20-foot edge. When the second magnetised his shield, he dropped the shield, abandoned an attempted shove, hit it two-handed and sent it over the edge as well.
5. From above, Thud pushed a roughly 500-pound rock onto a construct for 39 damage, then threw a smaller rock after it. Ember and the ranged attackers continued firing from the lip.
6. With the last construct burning and barely functional, Thud jumped the 20-foot drop, accepted the fall damage and killed it with a 24-damage maul strike. The impact scattered mechanical fragments into the quarry wall.

The two constructs yielded 40 gp of metal scrap each. Their attacks had cost Thud roughly 40 hit points before healing, validating the encounter's danger despite the later height advantage.

## Quarry workers and the ambush

On the lower shelf the party found three quarry workers sheltering behind stone: Jeff, young and shaking; Jory, older and holding a cold chisel as a weapon; and Sarah, whose forearms were burned. They had seen the Rammer investigate the party's noise, destroy a mule and cart, then withdraw toward its cave. They also described a longer route through the old ventilation workings, which the creature had avoided when the air system operated.

The party rested while planning an ambush. Thud selected a choke point, set two layers of hunting traps, camouflaged them with carpentry tools, placed the recovered blasting powder beside the route, fixed climbing ropes and kept a Molotov ready. Lucian planned to place an illusion near the traps and use Faerie Fire; Nova chose firing positions and prepared her force ballista. The workers were sent back toward the hoist camp. The long rest and trap preparation together occupied roughly 20 hours.

## Ironbark Rammer

![[Assets/Session 5 - Ironbark Rammer.jpg|The Ironbark Rammer emerges from its quarry cave.]]

Thud rang a quarry cart like a gong to draw out the [[Ironbark Rammer]]. Its front armour imposed disadvantage on attacks made from ahead, while its knees and rear remained vulnerable.

### Combat record

1. Nova's opening arrow was negated by the frontal disadvantage, but her force ballista hit. Lucian's Dissonant Whispers was resisted, so he gave Thud Bardic Inspiration.
2. The Rammer charged the illusion and stepped into the first hunting trap, stopping short of the party. Thud threw his Molotov at the nearby blasting powder. His first attack roll failed; he spent Heroic Inspiration, hit the tin and caused a 14-damage explosion.
3. Thud moved for a rear angle and missed with his longbow. Lucian then marked the Rammer with Faerie Fire.
4. The Rammer broke the first trap but immediately entered the second. Thud crossed the difficult ground to its unarmoured rear-left knee and dealt 17 damage with the first maul blow; his second missed. Nova's radiant arrow further damaged the same knee.
5. The Rammer turned to face Thud rather than spend its action escaping and struck him twice with its horns. Rage reduced the piercing damage.
6. Thud attempted to lasso the creature's neck, then withdrew that improvised attempt when it would have consumed his action and struck the knee again for 23 damage.
7. Ember frightened the Rammer and hit it with fire. Nova exploited the rear line of sight with a True Strike arrow and force ballista, delivering the killing attacks.

The party recovered 20 ironbark plates, valued at 75 gp each, and two ivory horn nubs. Thud and Ember each retained one nub as a trophy. The [[Belhaven Hunt]] contract promises 320 gp on return.

## Eastern workings

Ember had previously seen an enormous snake in the eastern drill gallery. After the Rammer fell, Thud entered first in search of another trophy. Nova's force ballista critically struck the snake for 21 force damage. Thud raged, used Reckless Attack and killed it in his first turn with two heavy maul blows. He took its tongue as a trophy; the skin was valued at 40 gp.

The gallery yielded an adamantine chisel tip valued at 65 gp and another drill-bit set valued at 35 gp. The party then entered a collapsed, unstable tunnel where two [[Seam Lurker|Seam Lurkers]] attacked from beneath the rubble.

![[Assets/Session 5 - Seam Lurker.jpg|A Seam Lurker bursts through the collapsed eastern workings.]]

Thud initially stayed on his rock and drank while Lucian fought one exposed lurker. As both creatures began burrowing, he moved down to place himself in front of Lucian and readied his maul. When a lurker surfaced beside him, the readied strike dealt 21 damage. He then absorbed a claw-and-poison attack, killed that lurker on his next turn and crossed to help Lucian and Nova, although his second swing struck rock. Nova killed the remaining lurker at point-blank range. Ember and Thud each recovered claws and teeth; one lot was clearly valued at 30 gp, while the other's amount is lost in transcription.

At the iron seep, Thud bottled a sample worth 40 gp. In the Rammer's lair, Ember found a failed-hunter cache containing two additional ironbark plates. The table assigned those plates to Thud, bringing the recovered total to 22.

## Return and advancement

The party returned to the hoist camp around midday. A courier delivered [[Grandmother's Grasp]] to Thud and handed over a broadsheet. The new weapon uses normal maul damage and can invoke Death Roll once per long rest when it hits a Large or smaller creature; the full confirmed mechanics are recorded on the item page. Only fragments of the displayed broadsheet were spoken aloud, so its claims are not recorded as canonical content.

The table awarded 2,300 XP for the Rammer, 2,200 for the two Rust Gnawers, 1,400 for the two Seam Lurkers and 1,100 for the snake: 7,000 XP total to each character as applied at the table. The party reached 14,100 XP and level 6.
