## Summary

Lucian, Thud and Ordrin abruptly experienced a modern talk show governed by an unseen host and audience. They could interact only through speech. Physical actions, weapons and magic had no observable effect. The show interrogated their decisions from Session 1, then ended without explanation.

## Observed Sequence

1. Immediately after the opening recap, the hunting blind was replaced by a polished studio with circular golden lights, chairs, a host's desk and an audible audience.
2. The host announced the programme as “The Show Must Go On” and introduced Thud, Lucian and Ordrin by name.
3. The host demonstrated detailed knowledge of the previous session: the bell frogs, Greyfen, Lucian's Faerie Fire, Ordrin striking Lucian's foot, the medicine, Tavia and Thud restraining Corrin.
4. Lucian claimed his Faerie Fire tactic had been intentional and argued that the result vindicated him.
5. Ordrin asked where they were, addressed the DM directly and attempted to evade personal questions.
6. The host asked whether Ordrin helped people from compassion or from a need to be useful.
7. Thud was challenged to explain why he protected people he claimed not to care about.
8. Ordrin attempted to grab and strike the host, drew his warhammer and cast Toll the Dead. None of these actions changed the set or affected the host.
9. The host moved toward a moral dilemma: whether the party would surrender Tavia if doing so saved Greyfen.
10. The show ended abruptly before the dilemma was resolved.

## Apparent Interaction Rules

- The three party members could hear and speak to the host and one another.
- Verbal interruptions and answers changed the host's responses.
- Physical interaction with the host or set did not produce an observable effect.
- Weapons and magic did not function in an observable way.
- The host claimed to speak before a live audience and repeatedly invoked its reactions.
- No vote, score, reward, punishment or audience-controlled outcome was directly demonstrated.
- The host possessed knowledge of prior events, including private party failures and motivations.

## Return and Residue

- All three remembered the experience afterward and believed the others had shared it.
- Tavia appeared unaware that anyone had vanished and was confused by their questions.
- Thud tested the hunting blind for inconsistencies; it felt completely real.
- Ordrin cast Detect Magic. No magical effect was found within 30 feet.
- It remains unknown whether the party moved, hallucinated, dreamed, received a transmission or experienced some other phenomenon.

## Player Theory

Lucian's player compared the event to _Dungeon Crawler Carl_ and suspects the party may be forced to perform under audience sentiment. This is player speculation only. The interlude demonstrated audience awareness, but not an audience-sentiment mechanic.

## Open Questions

- Why were only verbal interactions possible?
- Did time pass in the campaign world?
- Can the phenomenon observe events continuously?
- Will future appearances include voting, rewards, punishments or altered circumstances?
- Why did the programme use the campaign title?
