The foreman at the [[Northern Quarry]]. He met the party at the hoist camp after a heavily armoured charging beast drove workers from the upper quarry.

Brack reported that the creature had destroyed two retaining walls and three wagons, badly bruised one worker and stopped the site from earning money. It had four legs, iron-like shoulders and may have emerged from a fissure. The party's later Nature check suggested an ironbark charger, but Brack did not identify the species himself.

The Hunt contract allows the party to kill or capture the quarry breaker and retain recovered materials. Brack remained at the lower yard while the party moved upslope.
