![[Assets/Arlen Marr.png|Lieutenant Arlen Marr in military dress.]]

A lieutenant sent to command the Greyfen inspection and recover [[Tavia Rel]] and the [[Altered Dispatch Ledger]]. Marr served under [[Lord-Captain Edric Vane]] and expected Vane to punish him if he returned empty-handed.

At Greyfen's public hearing, Lucian dismantled Marr's mission and persuaded him to defect from Vane's service. Marr allowed his soldiers to choose whether to remain, then joined the party himself. During the following ferry attack, his heavy crossbow delivered the killing blow against the CR 5 Fen Mother.
