The short-haired harbourmaster of [[Belhaven]], wearing a dark harbour coat. She impounded [[Captain Oda Merrick]]'s damaged barge for hull inspection, coordinated safe unloading and called [[Pell]] to assess the Fen Mother materials. She prioritised keeping the working quay clear and preventing contamination.

![[Assets/Mara Harbourmaster.jpg|Mara, Belhaven's harbourmaster, at the working quay.]]

Her safety hold was not an accusation against the party. Repair liability remained unresolved. This Mara is not [[Mara]], the bartender in Greyfen.
