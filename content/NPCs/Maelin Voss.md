Greyfen's precise, restrained and exhausted acting administrator. Expecting military inspectors to search for a runaway, she closed the ferry landing even while a medicine barge waited offshore and sickness spread through town.

Under pressure from [[Lucian Vale]], [[Thud]] and [[Ordrin Emberkeg]], Voss ordered the medicine unloaded, gave the party a [[Badge of Authority]] and promised ten gold each if the inspectors failed to find their target by midday.

At the inspection, Lucian persuaded Voss to support a public hearing of [[Tavia Rel]]'s evidence. After the ledger was read, [[Brother Sen]] assumed civic leadership. Voss remained in Greyfen and paid the party, though the final amount should be confirmed against character sheets.
