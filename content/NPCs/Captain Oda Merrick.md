The captain of the ferry carrying the party away from Greyfen. Merrick kept the damaged vessel moving during the Fen Mother's attack and reluctantly slowed long enough for the party to haul the carcass aboard.

## Session 3 — Belhaven

Brought the damaged ferry safely into [[Belhaven]]. The harbourmaster held the barge for inspection and repairs. Oda confirmed that the crocodile caused the main hull damage; repair costs and responsibility remain unsettled. The interruption to Greyfen's supplies was described at session close as two days.
