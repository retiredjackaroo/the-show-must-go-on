A precise, enthusiastic anatomy and monster-material specialist of the [[Belhaven Hunt]]. He met the party at [[Belhaven]]'s quay, inspected the Fen Mother's wounds and arranged to move the remains to the hall for appraisal and sale through [[Copperpot and Company]]. He valued observable evidence over Lucian's embellishments and identified the decisive crossbow wound.

![[Assets/Pell.jpg|Pell examining a scale in the Hunt's processing workshop.]]

Pell describes himself as more planner than champion. He made a wand of magic missile from a cinder newt's glands. After the market fight he offered the party membership, accommodation and access to the Hunt's processing and crafting contacts.
