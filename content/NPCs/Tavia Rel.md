A young former military clerk with black hair, a long cloak and military clothing. She fled [[Greyfen]] after hearing that soldiers would arrive for an inspection and hid in the [[North Marsh Hunting Blind]].

Tavia held the party at crossbow point until [[Lucian Vale]] played a familiar company song and [[Ordrin Emberkeg]] explained that they carried medicine. Recognising them as former soldiers, she invited them inside.

Tavia stole the [[Altered Dispatch Ledger]], which proves that senior officers falsified casualty and supply records and tried to prevent magistrate review. After recovering it from the [[Greyfen Watchtower]], she authenticated it at a public hearing and left Greyfen with the party.

## Open Questions

- Which authority can act on her evidence without being silenced?
- What retaliation will follow the public reading of the ledger?
