![[Assets/Lucan Rhyse Portrait.png|Secretary Lucan Rhyse carrying a sealed letter and briefcase.]]

The hand-delivered [[Lucan Rhyse's Invitation]] identifies him as secretary and appointed representative of [[Lord-Captain Edric Vane]]. The proposed meeting was at the military liaison's house; he offered a different Belhaven venue and safe passage if the invitees preferred it. The later hearing took place at the [[Belhaven Open Bell Sanctuary]].

A senior administrative representative of the [[Royal Army of Aldren]] in [[Belhaven]], Rhyse arrived at the sanctuary with a briefcase and two unarmed clerks, saying that his office records Lucian, Ordrin and Thud as absent without leave, Tavia as a clerk who departed with unreconciled documents, and Marr as the officer sent to recover her.

At the hearing, Rhyse said he acted for the wider military and the Crown rather than Vane personally. This differs from the invitation's description of his appointment; the scope of his authority remains unresolved. He recognised [[Vane's Warhammer]] as Vane's weapon but had no theft report for it.

After the party demonstrated the hammer's memory-erasing effect on a spider and repeated its testimony under the sanctuary priest's Zone of Truth, Rhyse agreed to compare the [[Altered Dispatch Ledger]] and accompanying copy with official records and interview other military witnesses. He received a copy; the party retained the originals.

Rhyse offered immediate discharge if the party surrendered every version of the documents and Tavia and Marr returned to military custody. The party refused. He then offered honorable discharge and benefits after investigation even if the evidence did not prove wrongdoing, provided the party did not publicly spread unproven allegations. Lucian accepted only while the investigation remained fair and the party was protected from Vane.

Rhyse reported that Vane intended to reach Belhaven within three days to deliver news from operations in the far western marches. He later attended Lucian's dockside performance. After the barge exploded, he sent guards to find the people registered at the Hunt under the party's cover names.
