A soldier from [[Arlen Marr]]'s inspection force who chose to leave with the party after the Greyfen hearing. He was killed by the Fen Mother's tail while defending the ferry.

Not to be confused with **Sebastian Vale**, [[Lucian Vale]]'s recurring false identity.

## Session 3 — Belhaven

Others had forgotten Sebastian, while Lucian and Ordrin retained his memory. Ordrin's account restored him to Thud's memory; Lucian's later song at the [[Belhaven Open Bell Sanctuary]] restored remembrance to listeners who had known him, including his wife. Ordrin informed her of his death and comforted her. His body was discovered aboard the damaged barge. Lucian promised the widow about 5 gp from salvage; payment was not recorded. Why he was forgotten remains unresolved: his established cause of death is the Fen Mother's tail, not a hammer strike.
