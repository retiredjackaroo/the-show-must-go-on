The party's trading-company cover, used to register in [[Belhaven]] and handle the sale of the party's existing Fen Mother loot materials. No salvage claim was made through the company. It was devised as a shell identity, not established as a pre-existing legitimate business.

Ordrin registered as Bram Copperpot, Thud as Hobb Miller, Tavia as Nessa Finch, Marr as Darren Holt and Jon as John Smith. Lucian used Sebastian Vell/Vel, a travelling merchant and antiques broker; exact surname spelling awaits confirmation. These covers are aliases of existing people, not additional characters.
