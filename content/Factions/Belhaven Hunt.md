A hunting guild in [[Belhaven]] that arranges monster contracts, preserves salvage, processes materials for a fee and connects hunters with magical-item crafters. Its old stone guild hall stands against the town's outer wall beside a creature market. [[Pell]] is an anatomy specialist and representative.

![[Assets/Session 3 - Belhaven Hunt Hall.jpg|The Belhaven Hunt hall, filled with trophies, hunters and contract boards.]]

Members' motives vary: some seek trophies and difficult contests, others valuable organs and magical materials. Live capture can avoid the cost of bringing a salvage specialist into the field. This practice does not establish that every captured animal is a threat.

The market displayed several creatures whose names were not established in play, alongside cinder newts and chainhide prowlers. Their appearance does not establish abilities, classification or provenance.

![[Assets/Session 3 - Quilled Market Creature.jpg|An unnamed quilled creature displayed at the Hunt market.]]

![[Assets/Session 3 - Cinder Newt.jpg|A heat-shedding cinder newt displayed at the Hunt market.]]

![[Assets/Session 3 - Iridescent Market Bird.jpg|An unnamed iridescent bird displayed at the Hunt market.]]

The party was offered membership after stopping the escaped prowlers and used the hall's lodging and crafting contacts. Guests can stay through a member, though merchant access is more complicated. Exact individual enrolment was not recorded.

The Hunt crafted [[Grandmother's Grasp]] and sent it to Thud by courier at the [[Northern Quarry]] after Session 5.

In Session 4, the party took the sheets for all three posted contracts: The Quarry Breaker, Teeth in the Orchard and The Eastern Cry. They attempted The Quarry Breaker first and reached the Northern Quarry without a field extraction or capture expert. In Session 5 they killed the [[Ironbark Rammer]], recovered 22 ironbark plates and completed the contract. The Hunt's 320 gp payment remained unclaimed at session end. Teeth in the Orchard and The Eastern Cry remain outstanding.
