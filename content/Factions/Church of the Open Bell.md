In [[Session 3 - Notes]], the order's [[Belhaven Open Bell Sanctuary]] offered shelter through [[Karad Ren]]. Its faith honours names, memory and the dead, and abhors the soul-consuming effects of [[Vane's Warhammer]]. The teaching described in play emphasises answering a calling to do good and takes a dim view of desertion. Karad nevertheless backed the party after hearing its evidence and seeing the weapon.

The deity named in play is [[Aestra, the Witness]].
