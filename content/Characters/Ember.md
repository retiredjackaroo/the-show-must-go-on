Ember is a human sorcerer whose subclass is Draconic Sorcery. She joined Lucian Vale, [[Thud]] and [[Nova]] at the [[Northern Quarry]] in Session 5.

**Status:** Level 6 after Session 5

**First appears:** [[Session 5 - Narrative|Session 5]]

## Appearance

Ember is five feet seven inches tall. When she appeared at the quarry she wore crudely made hide clothing, carried no visible weapon and had wild, untidy hair. Small golden scales cover part of her chest and torso. Arrows from Lucian and Thud struck those scales and glanced away.

## Background and Purpose

Ember said she grew up far away with her mother, whom she identifies as a dragon. She repeatedly called herself a young dragon during the quarry expedition, but her canonical species is human; treat “dragon” as her self-description and draconic heritage, not a species correction.

According to Ember, a dragon she called Vexilfrax once tolerated other dragons in his territory before destroying their lairs, scattering their families and driving survivors into exile. Her mother was among those survivors. The exiled dragons have studied ruins and failed expeditions for centuries, looking for a way to defeat or change Vexilfrax.

Ember reported that they had scryed [[Vane's Warhammer]], watched [[Lord-Captain Edric Vane]] absorb power through it and concluded that it was made from the essence of an elder beast she called “the Unmade.” She came to learn what happened to that beast, how the hammer works and how Vane is involved. She hopes that the answer may reveal a way to act against Vexilfrax.

These are Ember's claims. They have not been independently confirmed. The provisional “Unmade” name must not replace the established [[Beast of Forgotten Souls]] record unless later evidence reconciles them. The spelling of Vexilfrax also remains to be confirmed.

## Observed Magic

- Golden draconic scales deflected two arrows during her introduction.
- Cast Friends on Thud.
- Cast Fire Bolt against quarry enemies.
- Used Feather Fall to protect herself and Nova during a descent.
- Activated Innate Sorcery before fighting the [[Ironbark Rammer]].
- Induced fear in the Rammer and a [[Seam Lurker]].
- Used controlled flight or gliding movement in the quarry.
- Used a ring to create a small flame for light.
- Produced a healing potion from her component pouch and gave it to Thud.

Exact rules and sources for individual effects were not fully reconciled at the table.

## Session 5 — Northern Quarry

After surviving Lucian and Thud's reflexive arrow fire, Ember introduced herself and explained her interest in Vane's hammer. She joined the group as it descended through the quarry.

Against the [[Rust Gnawer|Rust Gnawers]], she attacked with Fire Bolt and used Feather Fall to protect Nova. She later gave Thud a healing potion. During the Rammer ambush she activated Innate Sorcery, attacked with fire and frightened the beast. Afterward, she revealed the enormous snake in the eastern gallery, lit the tunnels, helped fight the Seam Lurkers and found both an adamantine chisel tip and a failed-hunter cache containing two ironbark plates.

Session-end XP: 14,100; level 6 was granted.

## Relationships

- **[[Thud]]:** Shot at Ember on sight and remained wary, then accepted her healing and relied on her light in the quarry. They each kept an ivory nub from the Rammer.
- **[[Nova]]:** The first party member Ember regarded as kind. Ember followed her during the early quarry descent and protected her with Feather Fall.
- **[[Lucian Vale]]:** Shot at and mocked Ember during their introduction. She remained openly unimpressed.
