![[Assets/Nova.png|Nova, the party's constructed artificer and former company artillerist.]]

A former company artillerist who joined the party in [[Session 3 - Narrative]]. Nova fought from a rooftop with a force ballista and radiant arrows, then helped identify and contain [[Vane's Warhammer]].

Nova stayed loyal to Vane during the mutiny but now seeks answers alongside the party. Nova reports Vane's death while acknowledging hazy memories; this remains testimony, not a resolution of Vane's apparent survival. Proximity to the hammer worsened the fog.

Nova reached level 5 at the end of Session 4 with 7,100 XP. The following statistics are preserved from the last reconciled level-4 sheet rather than presented as current level-5 values: proficiency bonus +2, 31 hit points, AC 17 and a walking speed of 30 feet. The sheet recorded these ability scores: Strength 10 (+0), Dexterity 14 (+2), Constitution 14 (+2), Intelligence 19 (+4), Wisdom 12 (+1) and Charisma 8 (-1).

Saving throws are Strength +0, Dexterity +2, Constitution +4, Intelligence +6, Wisdom +1 and Charisma -1. Nova's listed skills are Acrobatics +2, Animal Handling +1, Arcana +6, Athletics +0, Deception -1, History +4, Insight +1, Intimidation -1, Investigation +6, Medicine +1, Nature +4, Perception +3, Performance -1, Persuasion -1, Religion +4, Sleight of Hand +4, Stealth +4 and Survival +1. Proficiencies include light and medium armour, shields, simple weapons, blowgun, hand and heavy crossbows, longbow, musket, net and pistol; cartographer's, jeweller's, smith's, thieves', tinker's and woodcarver's tools; Common, Common Sign Language and Dwarvish. The sheet lists Poison and Magical Sleep under defenses.

The confirmed attack is a Repeating Shot Longbow: +5 to hit, 1d8+3 damage, range 150/600, with the replicated-item, martial, ammunition, heavy, two-handed and slow properties. Nova's listed Artificer features and actions are Create Eldritch Cannon, Tinker's Magic (four uses per long rest), Unarmed Strike, Two-Weapon Fighting and the Eldritch Cannon modes Flamethrower (DC 14 Dexterity save, 2d8+4 fire), Force Ballista (2d8 force and up to 5 feet of push) and Protector (1d8+4 temporary hit points). Listed spells are Jump, Shield and Mending; ritual spells are Alarm, Detect Magic and Identify. The full feature text remains on the source sheet, while this page records the campaign-relevant mechanics.

Nova's extradimensional bag contained the hammer overnight. At the church it was removed and placed on the floor; repacking was not recorded. Nova also expressed interest in observing the crafting of [[Grandmother's Grasp]].

## Session 4 — Belhaven and the Northern Quarry

Corroborated the order to attack the Elder Dragon during [[Lucan Rhyse]]'s hearing, while the sanctuary priest recorded that Zone of Truth did not affect her. Helped ignite the barge from a rooftop and used a contact-powder arrow to divert a guard without injuring him.

On the road, [[What the Fuck Is Going On]] appeared and Nova disclosed that she had previously been principal cast, failed, was recast as the toad Lucian compelled to face the Fen Mother, and later became a Star again through that sacrifice. She said she carries Nova's memories and the toad's memories. Her Star Power was disclosed as 60 with a +12 modifier.

At the quarry, Nova took a payroll script and 30 gp intended for the workers. Her Thunderwave struck both [[Bucket Scrabs]] swarms and pushed them down the ledge. She ended the session with the production guide and [[Vane's Warhammer]] stored in her extradimensional bag. Session-end XP: 7,100; level 5 was granted immediately.
