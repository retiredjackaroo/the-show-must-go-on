A surface quarry about half a day's travel from [[Belhaven]], reached by the northern quarry road. The [[Belhaven Hunt]] posted the Quarry Breaker contract after an armoured charging beast disrupted the workings.

The lower hoist camp contains an A-frame, tarped crates, spoil berms and chalk tallies on exposed rock. A steep switchback climbs past three wrecked wagons to a mid-bench ledge, scree gully and an old crane above a spoil ditch. Grinding stone can be heard from the upper quarry.

[[Foreman Brack]] reported two retaining walls and three wagons destroyed. One worker remained badly bruised. The likely ironbark charger appears to have emerged from a fissure and travelled repeatedly along the upper road.

In Session 4, the party followed one set of charger tracks, fought two swarms of [[Bucket Scrabs]] at the mid-bench and toppled the crane into the ditch to end the battle. A [[Surveyor's Compass]] was found where the crane had stood.

In Session 5, Ember joined Lucian, Thud and Nova at the quarry. The group descended through a retaining breach, destroyed two [[Rust Gnawer|Rust Gnawers]] and found three surviving quarry workers on the lower shelf. After a long rest and extensive preparation, [[Thud]] lured the [[Ironbark Rammer]] into two lines of hunting traps and a blasting-powder ambush. The party killed the Rammer and recovered 20 plates from its carcass.

The eastern workings include a timbered drill gallery, a collapsed unstable tunnel, an iron seep, an overlook and the Rammer's lair. The party killed an enormous unnamed snake and two [[Seam Lurker|Seam Lurkers]], found valuable drill fittings, bottled an iron-seep sample and recovered two more ironbark plates from a failed-hunter cache. They returned to the hoist camp after completing the Quarry Breaker contract. No active quarry threat remained established at session end, although an optional harder encounter in the lair was not engaged.
