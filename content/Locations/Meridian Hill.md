Meridian Hill lies two days east of [[Belhaven]] and is the site of the [[Belhaven Hunt]] contract called the Eastern Cry. It rises above abandoned pasture to a ruined observatory whose broken stone ring, brass meridian arm and open dome stand on the crown.

Roughly six weeks before Session 6, an unnatural blizzard settled over the hill and a brass-edged animal cry began moving through the valleys. The former grazing country emptied. At the end of Session 6, a separate war-horn sound came from the summit and temporarily silenced the Cry, wind and blizzard. Session 7 established the source as the [[Cry Wildheart]]. Its death ended the blizzard again.

## Known route

- **Shepherd cairn:** The last clear trail fork. Warning carvings identify [[Chart Rider|Chart Riders]], [[Rime Stalker|Rime Stalkers]] and [[Gap Siphon|Gap Siphons]].
- **Trail camp:** A sheltered saddle where Kelm and Juno were preparing to leave. The party defended it from twelve Chart Riders. The wagon cannot travel beyond this point.
- **Windcut Ridge:** An exposed trail with large predator tracks and a mutilated hill ram. Two Rime Stalkers attacked here.
- **Echo Gap:** An icy brass bridge over the glacier's source. The party cleared ten Gap Siphons and recovered Kelm's missing sheep beyond it.
- **Glacier Tongue:** Four-foot-thick clear ice over fast meltwater. The party lost the [[Ear-Notch Echo Catcher]] beneath it.
- **Cliff shelf:** A [[Lament Widow]] nested here and held Rusk. The party rescued the dog and recovered one missing Hunt tag below the ledge.
- **Broken wall and instrument yard:** The approach to the crown contains old brass instruments, bird nests, a stair to the cistern and dormant Meridian Wards.
- **Meridian ring:** The [[Cry Wildheart]] fought the frost giant Thurnvold here for almost a day. The party killed Thurnvold after joining the battle.
- **Observatory:** A multi-level complex containing Meridian Wards, small human-shaped wretches, lower service spaces, old charts and machinery. The party passed directly through it to kill the Cry Wildheart on the roof. During their long rest, the observatory reactivated. It remains uncleared.

Juno requested a meridian-arm blueprint, journals and charts, the fate of an earlier Hunt group and one deactivated construct guard. Two of three Hunt tags have been recovered.
