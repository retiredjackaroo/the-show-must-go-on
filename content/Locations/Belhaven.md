A lakeside town on the eastern shore reached by [[Captain Oda Merrick]]'s barge from [[Greyfen]]. Pale stone buildings and red-brown roofs climb above a busy quay toward a square bell tower. The lake's name and Belhaven's wider administrative placement remain unspecified.

![[Assets/Session 3 - Belhaven Aerial.jpg|Belhaven's walls, harbour, market and surrounding roads from above.]]

[[Mara (Harbourmaster)|Mara]] controls the harbour. The [[Belhaven Hunt]] occupies a stone guild hall near the outer wall, with a market, creature cages and processing facilities. The [[Belhaven Open Bell Sanctuary]] stands above the harbour.

![[Assets/Session 3 - Belhaven Market.jpg|The market square near the Hunt during the arrival of the Fen Mother salvage.]]

At the end of [[Session 3 - Notes]], the barge was under repair and its Greyfen supply service interrupted. A military liaison has premises in town, but the party rejected those premises for the offered meeting with Vane's representative.
