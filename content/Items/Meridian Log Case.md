# Meridian Log Case

A rare, non-attunement wondrous item recovered from the animal-wheel cache at the Crown Observatory. It is a slim, waterproof document case bound in brass and covered with intricate star patterns.

## Properties

- Its carrier has advantage on Investigation checks to find documents, seals, hidden ledges, false bottoms and markings in archives, offices, libraries and ruins.
- Once per long rest, its carrier can cast Comprehend Languages without components, targeting writing they are touching. The effect lasts for one hour.
- Papers stored inside are protected from water.
- Documents placed within appear to vanish from casual inspection. A person who knows a document is present can open the case and retrieve it normally.

Nova found two hidden [[Meridian Star Charts]] inside the case. The party now stores all seven known charts in it.
