A hand-delivered letter addressed to [[Karad Ren]] at the [[Belhaven Open Bell Sanctuary]]. It is signed “Secretary Lucan Rhyse” and identifies him as the appointed representative of [[Lord-Captain Edric Vane]]. The letter asks Karad to convey an invitation to [[Lucian Vale]], [[Thud]], and [[Ordrin Emberkeg]]; [[Arlen Marr]], [[Tavia Rel]], and Jon Hay are also named as invitees. Karad is invited to witness the conference.

The proposed meeting was at the military liaison's house on the following day at the second afternoon bell, with another Belhaven venue offered if the invitees or Church preferred. Rhyse promised no detention or compelled surrender of documents, weapons, or testimony during the conference, and safe passage for every named attendee. He proposed hearing the party's account and having its records compared with the complete military archive by an independent authority. These were commitments in the invitation, not proof that every term was later fulfilled.

The actual Session 4 hearing took place at the sanctuary. The letter names Jon Hay as invited; his attendance is not confirmed. Its description of Rhyse as Vane's appointed representative contrasts with Rhyse's later statement that he acted for the wider army and Crown, a question of authority that remains unresolved.
