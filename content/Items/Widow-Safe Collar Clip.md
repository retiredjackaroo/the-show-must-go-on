The Widow-Safe Collar Clip is an uncommon, weightless wondrous item that does not require attunement. Kelm gave it to the party after Rusk was returned alive in Session 7.

The small brass clip is shaped like a closed shepherd's eye and holds a twist of faded ribbon. Once per long rest, its wearer can use a soft word to cast Animal Friendship on one visible beast within 30 feet. A willing beast wearing the clip has advantage on saving throws against being frightened or charmed. A person wearing it has advantage on Wisdom (Animal Handling) checks and on saving throws against being frightened by beasts or monstrosities.

Thud retained ownership but clipped it to Lucian Vale.
