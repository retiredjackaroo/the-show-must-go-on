# Deactivated Meridian Ward

An intact, inactive construct guard recovered from the Crown Observatory at [[Meridian Hill]]. It weighs 150 lb and has no recorded sale value.

[[Belhaven Hunt]] requested one deactivated guard as part of Juno's observatory commission. The party secured this ward in Nova's extradimensional bag after clearing the chart vault, but it has not yet been delivered.
