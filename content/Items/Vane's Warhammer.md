A beautifully made adamantine light hammer taken from [[Lord-Captain Edric Vane|Lord-Captain Vane's]] campaign chest during the mutiny of [[The Company That Wouldn't Die]]. [[Lucian Vale]] took it; [[Thud]] carried and wielded it before its Session 3 containment.

The original inventory described it as a light hammer; see the Session 3 discrepancy below. Its adamantine head is exceptionally hard: a hit against an object is a critical hit.

**Latest custody:** Revealed at the Belhaven sanctuary after transport in Nova's bag

## Necromantic origin

The hammer is an ancient necromantic artefact fashioned using the remains or essence of an elder beast known as the [[Beast of Forgotten Souls]]. Its construction binds three effects together: the victim's death, the erasure of that victim from living memory, and the transfer of the victim's soul into the wielder as power.

Those accumulated souls can restore the wielder to life after death. The revelation also raised the possibility that prolonged use allowed [[Lord-Captain Edric Vane]] to acquire a similar power himself, but the party has not established whether this happened or whether it explains the reports of his survival.

## Session 3 revelations

[[Nova]] identified the hammer's effects: killing a target erases memories of that victim and transfers soul power into the wielder. The weapon itself held no souls when examined. With sufficient power, a wielder can return to life one hour after death; it restores the wielder, not a chosen other person.

Thud killed an unconscious bellfrog to test it. The merchants, Lucian and Nova forgot the frog, while Ordrin understood what had happened. The DM described Thud's gain as one sixteenth of the power needed for his own return. The encounter does not establish a universal cost formula for other wielders or victims.

Ordrin's deity condemned the effect. Retelling Sebastian's life restored Thud's memory; Lucian's later song restored memories among listeners who had known Sebastian. The limits of these protections remain unknown. Sebastian died to the Fen Mother, so his erasure is not explained by a confirmed hammer kill.

**Latest custody:** Nova contained it overnight in an extradimensional bag. Thud removed it at the Belhaven sanctuary and placed it on the floor for Karad Ren. Repacking was not narrated. Karad wants it destroyed, but no method has been established.

## Weapon-profile discrepancy

The older record below identifies an adamantine light hammer; Session 3 repeatedly treats the weapon as a large maul. Retain the historical profile for provenance, but do not treat its damage, weight or properties as the current confirmed sheet until the DM or inventory settles the discrepancy.
