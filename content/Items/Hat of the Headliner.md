The **Hat of the Headliner** is a wondrous item with Narrative Accommodation provenance. It requires attunement by [[Lucian Vale]]. Its supernaturally produced properties and effects share that provenance.

> “Lucian Vale has always owned this hat.”

## Original Material

When Lucian performs an original song about a significant event he personally experienced, he has Advantage on the Charisma (Performance) check. After the performance, the song gains Popularity according to its reception.

| Performance check | Reception             | Popularity gained |
| ----------------: | --------------------- | ----------------: |
|        9 or lower | Flop                  |                +0 |
|             10–14 | Good Reception        |                +1 |
|             15–19 | Crowd Favourite       |                +2 |
|             20–24 | Hit                   |                +3 |
|             25–29 | Smash Hit             |                +4 |
|               30+ | Legendary Performance |                +5 |

A song can gain Popularity only once per district, meaningful audience. Repeated play for essentially the same audience does not increase Popularity.

## Back Catalogue and spread

When one of Lucian's original songs reaches **3 Popularity**, it enters his Back Catalogue. Each Back Catalogue song tracks Popularity separately.

| Popularity | Status            | Circulation effect                                                                                                                                                      |
| ---------: | ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|        3–5 | Local Song        | Circulates around the community in which it became popular                                                                                                              |
|        6–9 | Popular Song      | Begins appearing in places where Lucian has not personally performed it                                                                                                 |
|      10–14 | Regional Hit      | Lucian has Advantage on Charisma (Performance) checks for that song even if it would not qualify as Original Material; NPCs throughout its circulation may recognise it |
|      15–19 | Famous Song       | Ordinary NPCs in circulating areas may recognise Lucian as its original performer                                                                                       |
|        20+ | Cultural Standard | Enters popular culture; people may know its lyrics, refrain or tune even when they do not know Lucian                                                                   |

Popularity does not normally decrease unless extraordinary circumstances cause the song to disappear from circulation.

## Royalties

At the end of each session, Lucian receives royalties from every song in his Back Catalogue. Royalties are calculated per song using Lucian's level and that song's Popularity status at the end of the session.

| Lucian level | Local Song | Popular Song | Regional Hit | Famous Song | Cultural Standard |
| -----------: | ---------: | -----------: | -----------: | ----------: | ----------------: |
|          5–8 |      10 gp |        25 gp |        50 gp |      100 gp |            250 gp |
|         9–12 |      25 gp |        50 gp |       100 gp |      250 gp |            500 gp |
|        13–16 |      50 gp |       100 gp |       250 gp |      500 gp |          1,000 gp |
|        17–20 |     100 gp |       250 gp |       500 gp |    1,000 gp |          2,500 gp |

The income represents circulation through other performers, taverns, broadsheets, sheet music, licensing, tips and similar uses. Lucian does not need to collect it; Production ensures it reaches him.

## Songs Remember

If a Back Catalogue song prominently preserves the name, identity or story of a creature affected by supernatural forgetting or memory erasure, the song can begin resisting that erasure as it spreads.

| Song status       | Memory effect                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Local Song        | **Fleeting Recognition.** A creature hearing Lucian perform may experience vague familiarity with the forgotten creature.                                                                    |
| Popular Song      | **Fragments Return.** A creature hearing the song can make a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw; on a success, it remembers one significant fact it previously knew.                                  |
| Regional Hit      | **Remembrance.** On a successful DC 15 Wisdom saving throw, the creature's suppressed memories return for 24 hours.                                                                          |
| Famous Song       | **Restoration.** On a successful DC 15 Wisdom saving throw, the creature's suppressed memories return permanently unless a responsible supernatural effect prevents restoration.             |
| Cultural Standard | **Folk Memory.** The forgotten creature becomes embedded in communal memory; ordinary supernatural effects can no longer completely erase it from communities where the song is established. |

**Narrative Accommodation upgrade DC:** 20.
