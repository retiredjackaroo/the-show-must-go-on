A water-stained ledger stolen by [[Tavia Rel]]. Original entries and visible clerk initials prove that senior officers rewrote casualty counts, concealed a command-sealed relief convoy diversion and ordered records and authenticating witnesses secured before civil review.

[[Brother Sen]] read the ledger publicly at Greyfen. The party carried it away to seek higher authority.

## Session 3 — Belhaven

Presented to [[Karad Ren]] in Belhaven with supporting testimony and papers. The military response calls the discrepancies clerical errors and offers corrected figures and witnesses. Karad supports investigation after the hammer revelation; the competing explanation has not been adjudicated.
