A rare wondrous item requiring attunement, identified by [[Nova]] while it remained frozen inside the Glacier Tongue on [[Meridian Hill]]. It resembles a shepherd's ear tag or dream catcher made from brass, bone, gut cord, wire and a bead of frozen resin. It is delicate and flammable.

While worn, it reveals whether a sound heard within 60 feet is real, a natural echo, a magical duplicate or a mixture. It has two charges and regains one expended charge at dawn.

- **Mute the Metal:** As a bonus action, touch a metal object and create magical silence in a 5-foot sphere centred on it for one minute without concentration.
- **Steady Nerves:** Spend one charge for advantage on saving throws against being frightened.

The party freed the catcher from the surrounding ice, but the current carried it beneath the glacier before Ember could seize it. Its name and properties are transcribed from the DM's spoken description and should be checked against any later item card.
