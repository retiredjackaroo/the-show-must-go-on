An uncommon wondrous item that does not require attunement, given to the party by Kelm after they defended the [[Meridian Hill]] trail camp.

While lit, it sheds bright light for 20 feet and dim light for a further 40 feet. Its flame cannot be extinguished by nonmagical wind or magical gusts, although water, smothering and magical darkness still work. A creature carrying the lantern, or keeping it hung within 5 feet, has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) and Wisdom (Survival) checks made in heavy precipitation, fog or strong wind.

Once per long rest, **Caretaker's Light** casts Light on the lantern without concentration for one hour.
