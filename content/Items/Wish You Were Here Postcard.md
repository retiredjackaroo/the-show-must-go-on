# Wish You Were Here Postcard

A rare magical postcard recovered from the chart vault at [[Meridian Hill]]. Its front shows a sunlit beach hotel. The back reads, “Wish you were here. XOXO. Call me,” and smells of salt and citrus perfume.

## Properties

- **Hotel Coordinates:** While the postcard remains magical, its holder and anyone shown the card treat the beach hotel as a destination they have seen for teleportation magic. The coordinates are magical and do not express physical distance or direction.
- **Call Me — once per long rest:** Casts Sending without components, targeting either the writer or the hotel's front desk. If the writer is dead or unreachable, the desk may answer instead.
- **Wish You Were Here — once:** While outdoors under open sky, the holder and up to five willing creatures within ten feet arrive at the hotel's front steps without mishap. The postcard then becomes blank and mundane, and its stored coordinates are lost.

The party used Call Me and reached an older woman named Sally, who mistook Thud for Jeremy and said she was still waiting for Jeremy at the hotel. The observatory had been abandoned for roughly forty years.

The one-use teleport has not been spent.
