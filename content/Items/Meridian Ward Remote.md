# Meridian Ward Remote

A small brass control recovered at the Crown Observatory on [[Meridian Hill]]. Its play and pause controls can activate or suspend a particular Meridian Ward.

Nova used one remote to shut down a hostile ward temporarily while the party cleared the chart vault. The party recovered three remotes in total, valued at 25 gp each.

Each remote controls a particular ward rather than every construct in the observatory.
