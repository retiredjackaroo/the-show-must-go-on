# Echo Ward Schematic

A crafting diagram recovered from the chart vault at [[Meridian Hill]]. The plans concern echo- and sonic-based items and visibly call for material from the [[Cry Wildheart]].

## Properties

- Using the schematic reduces the labour cost of a suitable crafting project by 50 gp.
- Its hearing mode can be used once per long rest.

The exact items that can be produced from the schematic, and the practical limits of its hearing mode, have not yet been established.

The schematic was valued at 70 gp in the party's post-session accounting.
