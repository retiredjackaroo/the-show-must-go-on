The ivory Ironbark horn trophy carried by [[Thud]]. It awakened from the provisional “Horn of the Ironbark Charger” during Thud's charge against a [[Rime Stalker]] in Session 6.

If Thud moves at least 20 feet in a straight line toward a creature and hits it with a melee weapon attack on the same turn, he may activate the horn. The attack deals an additional 2d6 bludgeoning damage. The target must then fail a Strength saving throw to be pushed 15 feet directly away; the first activation's push succeeded automatically. Thud continues in the same direction with the target.

The transcript did not establish the saving-throw DC, activation frequency, attunement or whether the horn must be physically incorporated into the weapon.
