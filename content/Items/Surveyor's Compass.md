A magical compass found beneath the former crane mount at the [[Northern Quarry]] after the party toppled the structure onto the last [[Bucket Scrabs]]. It has two hands: one always points true north, while the other can point toward an object the bearer has previously seen if that object is within the spell's range.

The Session 5 opening recap clarified that it can cast Locate Object once per day and that the compass continues pointing without the bearer maintaining concentration. The spell's normal restrictions still apply. The exact range and procedure for changing the target were not read into the transcript.

The DM previously described it as a tool for measuring exact angles, assisting with underground mapping and detecting magical or planar anomalies. Its final carrier remains unresolved.
