A precision instrument found beneath the former crane mount at the [[Northern Quarry]] after the party toppled the structure onto the last [[Bucket Scrabs]].

The DM described it as a tool for measuring exact angles, assisting with underground mapping and detecting magical or planar anomalies. Lucian read an additional mechanical benefit from the displayed item card, but that rule was not confirmed aloud by the DM and is not recorded here as canonical.

Its final carrier and exact mechanics remain unresolved.
