A magical maul commissioned for [[Thud]] through the [[Belhaven Hunt]], using the entire Fen Mother skull and 100 gp of additional materials paid by Thud.

**Status:** Commissioned in Session 3; delivery promised at the end of two further sessions, provisionally Session 5. It is not yet in use.

## Death Roll

Once per long rest, when Thud hits a Large or smaller creature, the jaw fitting may clamp shut. The target makes a Strength saving throw. On failure it takes an additional 2d6 piercing damage, falls prone and has speed 0 until the beginning of Thud's next turn. Thud may then drag it up to half his movement without reducing his own speed.

The weapon counts as magical. The save DC, final base profile and whether a separate base maul must be supplied were not settled. Do not assign a +1 bonus without a later ruling. Thud bought a separate +1 maul for use while waiting.
