A magical maul commissioned for [[Thud]] through the [[Belhaven Hunt]], using the entire Fen Mother skull and 100 gp of additional materials paid by Thud. It uses the normal maul damage profile: 2d6 bludgeoning damage, with no inherent bonus to attack or damage rolls.

**Status:** Delivered to [[Thud]] by courier at the [[Northern Quarry]] hoist camp after the Session 5 expedition. No attack or other use was recorded before the session ended.

## Death Roll

**Once per long rest**, when Thud hits a Large or smaller creature with this maul, he may invoke Death Roll without using an action. The target makes a **DC 15 Strength saving throw**.

- **Failure:** The target takes an additional 2d6 piercing damage from the jaw clamp, falls prone and has speed 0 until the start of Thud's next turn. Thud may drag the target up to half his walking speed; this drag does not cost his own movement.
- **Success:** The target takes half the piercing damage only. It does not fall prone and is not grabbed.

The weapon counts as magical but otherwise uses the normal maul profile. It is not a +1 weapon. Thud bought a separate +1 maul for use while waiting.
