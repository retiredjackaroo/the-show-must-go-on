# Meridian Star Charts

The Meridian Star Charts are a collection of astronomical records recovered from the observatory at [[Meridian Hill]]. Each numbered chart depicts the sun and five planets in a particular configuration: three planets follow broadly level orbital paths, while two move on perpendicular paths.

The party currently holds seven charts. They are stored together in the Meridian Log Case recovered from the observatory.

## Teleportation quest

The large ring outside the observatory is a disabled teleportation circle. Restoring it requires solving the puzzle represented by the charts, but the method and the complete number of charts required are not yet known.

The charts are not the key to the nearby wheel marked with the frog, snake, bug, dragon, paw and wing symbols. That was a separate mechanism, and the party confirmed that its animal symbols did not correspond to the planetary diagrams.

## Open questions

- What sequence or relationship connects the seven known planetary configurations?
- Are any charts still missing?
- How does the completed solution reactivate the teleportation circle?
- Where can the restored circle travel?
