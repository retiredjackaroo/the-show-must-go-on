# Braced Melt

The Braced Melt was a giant elemental of ice, water and rubble that occupied the Crown Observatory's cistern at [[Meridian Hill]]. Before confronting the party, portions of its living water moved through the observatory pipes and interfered with the boiler and lower chambers.

Two magical bronze braces reinforced the creature and granted it unusual resistance to damage. Nova and Ember destroyed the braces during the battle, after which its form became more like ordinary water and its resistances weakened.

The Melt flooded sections of the cistern chamber, soaked its targets, dragged Nova and Thud into the freezing basin and attacked them at close range. Lucian killed it with Dissonant Whispers after the party broke both braces.

Its defeat satisfied the observatory console's third and final threat condition, allowing the party to claim the complex as a bastion. The remains yielded alchemical material worth 120 gp. Thud kept a small valueless piece of brace metal as a trophy.
