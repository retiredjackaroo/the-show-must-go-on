The Beast of Forgotten Souls was an ancient elder beast whose remains or essence were used to fashion [[Vane's Warhammer]]. The weapon preserves the beast's necromantic relationship with death, memory and souls: a victim killed by it is forgotten, while the victim's soul passes into the wielder as power that can eventually restore the wielder to life.

The beast has not appeared directly. Its form, history, original abilities, death, and the identity of the hammer's maker remain unknown. Do not infer those details from the weapon's present effects.
