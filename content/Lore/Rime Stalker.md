Large wolf-like predators found on [[Meridian Hill]]. Their bite can knock a target prone, and they can exhale a cone of severe cold that also arrests movement. Two attacked the party on Windcut Ridge. One knocked Nova unconscious before Lucian revived her; Thud killed the other as it fled Ember's Fear. The surviving frightened beast ran into the Gap Siphons at Echo Gap and was killed by them.

Each recovered set of pelt and fangs was valued at 260 gp.
