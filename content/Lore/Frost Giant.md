Frost giants are enormous, dangerous beings associated in table lore with an Elder Beast called the Exile. Session 7 established only that they may be lone nomads who follow or venerate that progenitor; their wider culture remains unknown.

The party encountered a 21-foot frost giant fighting the [[Cry Wildheart]] on the meridian ring of [[Meridian Hill]]. This draft uses the provisional name Thurnvold; the transcript also renders it as Thurnvault, Thurvold and similar variants. He wielded a huge greataxe, threw stone and enemies, used freezing sweeps and sounded a war horn. Cold did not harm him.

Thud killed Thurnvold by breaking his knee, causing the giant to fall onto broken stone and snap his neck. Thud took one molar as a magical trophy; its complete properties remain to be established in play.
