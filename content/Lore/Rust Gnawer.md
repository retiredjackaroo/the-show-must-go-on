Rust Gnawers are dog-sized quarry automatons used as guard, cleanup and rock-working machines. Two approached along the retaining wall after [[Thud]] loudly announced that he had recovered blasting powder.

Their observed abilities included breaking quarry walls, magnetising a metal weapon or shield, inflicting poison and resisting ordinary physical damage. The two constructs seriously injured and poisoned Thud before he used a warhammer's pushing technique to drive both over a 20-foot ledge. From above he dropped and threw large rocks onto them, then jumped down and destroyed the last survivor with his maul.

Each construct yielded metal scrap valued at 40 gp.

The exact spoken creature name was distorted repeatedly in the transcript. “Rust Gnawer” is the clearest contextual form and should be confirmed against the DM's source material if available.
