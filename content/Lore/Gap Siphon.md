Large mosquito-like creatures nesting at the Echo Gap on [[Meridian Hill]]. A Siphon that charges at least 20 feet can drive its proboscis into a target, knock the victim prone and remain attached so later feeding no longer needs an attack roll.

Ten attacked the party after killing a fleeing [[Rime Stalker]]. Ember's Fireball destroyed four, Nova and Ember killed several at range, and Thud crushed those that reached the bridge. One embedded itself in Thud's chest; he tore off its wings and body, leaving only the head lodged in him. Ten recovered heads were valued at 120 gp total.
