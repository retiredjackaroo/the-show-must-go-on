Large eagle-like predators that circle [[Meridian Hill]] and attack lit camps. Twelve came for the trail-camp fire in Session 6. They flew in waves, struck with talons and kept attacking after the real fire was replaced by Lucian's illusion. The party killed eleven; one fled. Their recovered feathers were valued at 110 gp and their talons at 220 gp.
