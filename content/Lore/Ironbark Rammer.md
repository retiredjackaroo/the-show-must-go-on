![[Assets/Session 5 - Ironbark Rammer.jpg|The Ironbark Rammer in the Northern Quarry.]]

The Ironbark Rammer was the heavily armoured four-legged beast that halted work at the [[Northern Quarry]] and became the target of the [[Belhaven Hunt]]'s Quarry Breaker contract.

It had extremely strong frontal armour, vulnerable rear joints and a powerful charge. In combat it could realign its armoured front toward a chosen opponent and attack twice with its horns. It was strong enough to break a hunting trap's restraint but had to spend effort doing so.

The party lured it from its cave with a quarry-cart gong and fought it in an ambush prepared by [[Thud]]. Hunting traps stopped two successive advances, recovered blasting powder exploded beside it and repeated attacks damaged its rear-left knee. Thud held it in melee while Ember frightened it. [[Nova]] delivered the killing attacks from the unarmoured rear.

The carcass yielded 20 ironbark plates worth 75 gp each and two small ivory horn nubs. A failed-hunter cache in its lair contained two additional plates, bringing the recovered total to 22. The Hunt contract promises 320 gp on return.

**First appears:** Described in Session 4; encountered and killed in [[Session 5 - Narrative|Session 5]].
