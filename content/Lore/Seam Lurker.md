Seam Lurkers are medium elemental beasts that move beneath loose rock. Two attacked in a collapsed tunnel in the eastern workings of the [[Northern Quarry]].

They could burrow out of sight, burst from the rubble, attack with two claws and project a cone of iron dust. The dust caused poison damage and could impair attacks. Ember's fear effect worked on at least one lurker.

[[Thud]] readied his maul while the creatures were underground, struck one as it emerged and killed it on his next turn. [[Nova]] killed the other with a point-blank arrow. Their claws and teeth were salvageable; one recovered lot was valued at 30 gp, while the value of the other was obscured by the transcript.

**First appears:** [[Session 5 - Narrative|Session 5]].
