The Cry Wildheart was the winged, antlered creature responsible for the Eastern Cry and the unnatural blizzard over [[Meridian Hill]]. It nested on the ruined observatory roof.

The creature used thunderous cries, cutting wind and sudden aerial movement. Its full cry deafened and badly wounded the party; its wind attacks could drive creatures toward drops. It resisted much of Thud's first assault and repeatedly slipped away from attacks.

In Session 7 it fought the frost giant Thurnvold for almost a day, withdrew to its nest after the party killed the giant, then died to Nova's radiant arrow. Its death ended the blizzard. The party retained its antlers as proof for the [[Belhaven Hunt]], along with valuable membranes, cry glands and hide.
