# Crynord

The Crynord are the surviving human staff of the Crown Observatory at [[Meridian Hill]]. They have endured inside the abandoned complex for roughly forty years without ageing.

Their bodies remain human, but a powerful curse scattered their memories and concentration, leaving them confused, erratic and prone to aggression. They often scuttle away from visitors or hide among the ruins. One survivor, Jeremy, retained enough lucidity to describe the observatory's fall.

According to Jeremy, the astronomers saw planets moving incorrectly through the telescope. They reached out, encountered something outside their known gods and were noticed in return. The being cursed them after seeing them observe it.

Nova's Detect Magic supported the presence of a powerful external curse that halted ageing and damaged cognition. The [[Cry Wildheart]] imposed an additional degrading influence, but that effect began fading after the creature's death. Lucian's attempt to cast Dispel Magic produced a warning that he lacked the required administrative privileges.

The curse remains active and will require unusually powerful magic to break. After the party claimed the observatory as a bastion, the Crynord remained as residents and may help maintain it if they can be cured.
