A six-legged, cat-like creature with overlapping bronze scales and chains that form part of its body. The juvenile seen at the [[Belhaven Hunt]] market was about the size of a house cat; the adult was much larger.

![[Assets/Session 3 - Adult Chainhide Prowler.jpg|An adult chainhide prowler breaking free.]]

![[Assets/Session 3 - Juvenile Chainhide Prowler.jpg|A juvenile chainhide prowler with bronze overlapping scales.]]

The sedated adult broke containment upon seeing the caged juvenile. Its movement produced a frightening chain-rattle and it could rend and knock opponents down. Both animals died during the resulting market fight in [[Session 3 - Notes]]. Thud retained one scale. Their place in the setting's broader creature categories is not yet confirmed.
