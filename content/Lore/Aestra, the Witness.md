The deity honoured by the [[Church of the Open Bell]] in [[Belhaven]].

Session 3 establishes Aestra as a keeper of names and memory, also called she who hears and bearer of the open bell. Her order honours the dead and rejects the hammer's theft of souls and remembrance. The transcript's Astra is treated as a spelling variant of the established Aestra.
