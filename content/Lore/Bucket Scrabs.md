Fist-sized, rock-like bugs encountered in two swarms of roughly ten at the [[Northern Quarry]]. At rest or from a distance they resembled loose stones; closer observation revealed moving legs. They climbed the quarry walls and attacked by covering a target as a swarm.

Fire, Faerie Fire, Sacred Flame, a Thunderwave and Thud's maul reduced the swarms. Thud then toppled the failing crane into the spoil ditch, crushing the survivors. The DM awarded 450 XP for the encounter.

No wider habitat, origin, creature classification or relationship to the ironbark charger was established.
