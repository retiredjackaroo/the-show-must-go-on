A Lament Widow is a woman-shaped, winged harpy encountered on a cliff shelf of [[Meridian Hill]]. The Session 7 Widow nested among huge pale ribs tied with red cord and had captured Rusk, Kelm's sheepdog.

She could bargain and converse in Common. After Lucian Vale charmed her with Friends, she flirted openly with him, proposed a meal and “something more,” and invited him to become her pet; Lucian flirted back while negotiating Rusk's release. When the charm expired, she recognised the deception and attacked after Lucian failed to preserve their rapport through ordinary persuasion. Her lament inflicted psychic disorientation and could force listeners to move unpredictably. Lucian killed her after using invisibility and illusions to misdirect her.

The single encounter does not establish the habits or nature of all Lament Widows.
