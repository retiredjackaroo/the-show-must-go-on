A Lament Widow is a woman-shaped, winged harpy encountered on a cliff shelf of [[Meridian Hill]]. The Session 7 Widow nested among huge pale ribs tied with red cord and had captured Rusk, Kelm's sheepdog.

She could bargain and converse in Common, but treated the party as Lucian Vale's pets and turned hostile when challenged. Her lament inflicted psychic disorientation and could force listeners to move unpredictably. Lucian killed her after using invisibility and illusions to misdirect her.

The single encounter does not establish the habits or nature of all Lament Widows.
